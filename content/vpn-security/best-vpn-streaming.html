5 Best VPNs for Streaming in 2026 (All Platforms Tested)

Updated May 12, 2026 • 12 min read

Not all VPNs are created equal when it comes to streaming. Many VPNs that work perfectly fine for general privacy get instantly detected and blocked by Netflix, Disney+, and other streaming platforms. The ones that work today might not work tomorrow, because streaming services are constantly updating their VPN detection systems.

We tested five of the most popular VPNs specifically on streaming performance: which platforms they can access, how fast they are for HD and 4K content, and how reliably they work day after day. Here are the results.

1. NordVPN — Best Overall for Streaming

NordVPN consistently unblocked every streaming platform we tested, including multiple Netflix regional libraries, Disney+, Hulu, BBC iPlayer, Amazon Prime Video, and HBO Max. In our month of testing, we experienced zero detection failures on Netflix — a claim very few VPNs can make.

Speed is where NordVPN really pulls ahead for streaming. Using its NordLynx protocol (built on WireGuard), we measured average download speeds of 450+ Mbps on a 500 Mbps connection. That is more than enough for multiple simultaneous 4K streams without a hint of buffering. Even connecting to servers on the other side of the globe, speeds stayed above 200 Mbps.

NordVPN supports all major platforms and devices: Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Linux, Fire TV Stick, Android TV, and most smart TVs through its SmartDNS feature. The SmartDNS option is particularly useful for devices that do not support VPN apps natively, like older smart TVs and gaming consoles.

The app makes streaming easy even for VPN beginners. You can browse servers by country and connect with a single tap. NordVPN also labels servers that are optimized for streaming, so you do not have to guess which server will work best for Netflix US versus BBC iPlayer.

2. ExpressVPN — Fastest Speeds for Streaming

ExpressVPN is the premium pick for streamers who want the absolute fastest, most reliable experience. It costs more than competitors, but you get what you pay for: rock-solid connections that rarely buffer, even on distant servers.

In our speed tests, ExpressVPN delivered the most consistent performance across different server locations. While NordVPN occasionally had speed variations between servers, ExpressVPN maintained high speeds almost regardless of which server we connected to. For 4K streaming, this consistency matters more than raw peak speed.

Platform support is comprehensive. ExpressVPN has a dedicated app for Fire TV and Android TV, a MediaStreamer DNS feature for smart TVs and consoles, and browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox. The router app is particularly noteworthy — install it once on a compatible router and every device on your network gets VPN protection without individual setup.

ExpressVPN unblocked Netflix in 20+ countries during our testing, along with Disney+, Hulu, BBC iPlayer, Amazon Prime Video, and DAZN. The reliability was near-perfect, with only occasional hiccups on less popular regional libraries.

3. Surfshark — Best Budget Pick With Unlimited Devices

Surfshark proves that you do not need to spend a lot to get reliable streaming VPN access. At $2.49/month on the two-year plan, it is one of the most affordable options available — and it includes unlimited simultaneous device connections.

The unlimited device policy is a genuine differentiator for households. Instead of choosing which five or six devices get VPN access, every phone, tablet, laptop, smart TV, and streaming stick in your home can connect at once. For families that stream on multiple screens, this alone makes Surfshark worth considering.

In our streaming tests, Surfshark successfully unblocked Netflix (US, UK, Canada, Japan, and several other regions), Disney+, Hulu, and Amazon Prime Video. BBC iPlayer was less reliable — it worked about 80% of the time, compared to NordVPN's near-100% success rate.

Speeds were good but not class-leading. We averaged 300-350 Mbps on nearby servers, which is more than sufficient for 4K streaming. Distant servers dropped to 150-200 Mbps, which still handles HD without issues but might cause occasional buffering on 4K.

4. CyberGhost — Best Streaming-Optimized Servers

CyberGhost takes a unique approach to streaming: it maintains dedicated streaming-optimized servers labeled by the specific platform and country they are designed for. Instead of connecting to a random US server and hoping it works with Netflix, you select the "Netflix US" server and connect. This removes the guesswork entirely.

The dedicated streaming servers cover Netflix (multiple countries), Disney+, Hulu, BBC iPlayer, Amazon Prime Video, HBO Max, Peacock, and several regional services. CyberGhost's team actively monitors and updates these servers to stay ahead of platform VPN detection, which means the connection stays reliable over time.

With over 9,000 servers across 90+ countries, CyberGhost has the largest server network on this list. The sheer number of options means you are more likely to find a server geographically close to you, which helps with speed. In our tests, nearby servers delivered 350-400 Mbps, which handles 4K streaming without issues.

CyberGhost also offers a generous 45-day money-back guarantee (versus the 30-day standard), giving you extra time to test streaming performance with your specific setup.

5. Private Internet Access (PIA) — Best Customization for Streaming

Private Internet Access offers the most configurable streaming experience on this list. Power users will appreciate the ability to fine-tune encryption levels, protocols, DNS settings, and port selection to optimize for streaming performance. If you know your way around VPN settings, PIA lets you squeeze out every bit of speed.

PIA's streaming performance has improved significantly. It now reliably unblocks Netflix US, Disney+, Hulu, and Amazon Prime Video. BBC iPlayer access is hit-or-miss, similar to Surfshark. The app includes streaming-optimized server labels to help you find the right connection quickly.

At $2.03/month on the three-year plan, PIA is the most affordable option on this list. It also supports unlimited simultaneous device connections, matching Surfshark's offering at an even lower price point.

The trade-off is that PIA's app is less polished and beginner-friendly than NordVPN or ExpressVPN. The settings menus are more technical, and new users might feel overwhelmed by the options. But if you are comfortable with technology and want the most control over your VPN at the lowest price, PIA delivers strong value.

Which Streaming VPN Should You Choose?

Our Verdict

For the best all-around streaming experience: NordVPN is the top pick. It unblocks everything, delivers excellent speeds for 4K, and works across all devices.

For maximum speed and reliability: ExpressVPN justifies its premium price with the most consistent performance across all server locations.

For households on a budget: Surfshark's unlimited device connections and $2.49/mo price make it the best value for families who stream on multiple screens.

For ease of use: CyberGhost's platform-labeled streaming servers eliminate the guesswork of finding a working connection.

For power users on a budget: PIA offers unlimited connections and deep customization at the lowest price point.

How We Tested

We tested each VPN over four weeks, connecting daily to streaming platforms including Netflix (US, UK, Canada, Japan), Disney+, Hulu, BBC iPlayer, Amazon Prime Video, and HBO Max. We measured connection speeds using Speedtest.net before and during VPN usage, tested both HD and 4K content for buffering, and tracked daily unblocking success rates. All tests were performed on Windows, macOS, and Fire TV Stick.
