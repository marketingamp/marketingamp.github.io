What Is NordVPN?

NordVPN is a virtual private network service operated by Nord Security, a cybersecurity company based in Panama. It encrypts your internet traffic and routes it through servers in 111 countries, masking your real IP address and protecting your data from surveillance, hackers, and ISP tracking. Beyond basic VPN functionality, NordVPN has expanded into a broader security suite with threat protection, password management, and secure file storage.

NordVPN launched in 2012 and has grown to over 14 million users. It operates 6,400+ servers worldwide, making it one of the largest VPN networks available. Panama's jurisdiction is significant because the country has no mandatory data retention laws and is outside the 5/9/14 Eyes surveillance alliances.

Security and Privacy Features

NordVPN's security infrastructure is comprehensive and has been independently verified:

  • Encryption: AES-256-GCM encryption (military-grade, same standard used by governments)
  • Protocols: NordLynx (WireGuard-based, fastest), OpenVPN (UDP/TCP), and IKEv2/IPsec
  • No-logs policy: Independently audited by Deloitte (2022, 2023, 2024), confirming zero logging of user activity
  • RAM-only servers: All servers run in RAM-only mode — data is wiped on every reboot, making seizure-based data extraction impossible
  • Kill switch: Automatically blocks internet access if the VPN connection drops, preventing accidental data exposure
  • Double VPN: Routes traffic through two VPN servers for additional encryption layers
  • Onion over VPN: Combines VPN encryption with Tor network routing for maximum anonymity
  • DNS leak protection: Uses private DNS servers to prevent DNS queries from leaking to your ISP

We ran DNS leak tests, WebRTC leak tests, and IPv6 leak tests across Windows, macOS, and Android. NordVPN passed all tests with zero leaks detected. The kill switch activated reliably within seconds of connection drops during our testing.

It is not a replacement for dedicated antivirus software, but as an additional security layer, it is genuinely useful and something most competing VPNs do not offer.

Meshnet

Meshnet is a feature unique to NordVPN that lets you create a private encrypted network between your devices or share it with trusted friends and family. Think of it as a simplified, consumer-friendly version of a corporate VPN. You can route traffic through another device's connection, access files on your home computer remotely, or set up secure LAN gaming sessions.

Practical use cases include: accessing your home network while traveling (route traffic through your home PC for local IP access), setting up a secure connection for remote team collaboration, hosting LAN-style gaming parties across the internet, and sharing your VPN connection with family members on different devices.

Meshnet is included free with NordVPN and supports up to 10 of your own devices plus 50 external devices. It is a genuinely innovative feature that adds value beyond basic VPN functionality.

Apps and Device Support

NordVPN offers native apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, Android TV, and Fire TV. Browser extensions are available for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. You can also configure it manually on routers, smart TVs, and gaming consoles.

The desktop apps are well-designed with an interactive server map, one-click connect, and easy access to specialty servers (Double VPN, Onion over VPN, P2P-optimized). The mobile apps mirror the desktop experience closely and include a widget for quick connect/disconnect.

You get 10 simultaneous connections on a single account, which is enough for most households. If you need more, installing NordVPN on a router covers all devices on your network with a single connection slot.

Split Tunneling

Split tunneling lets you choose which apps or websites use the VPN connection and which use your regular internet. This is useful when you want to protect your browsing with the VPN while keeping local services like banking apps on your direct connection for compatibility.

NordVPN supports split tunneling on Windows, Android, and Android TV. macOS and iOS do not support it due to Apple platform restrictions (this limitation applies to most VPN providers, not just NordVPN). The feature works reliably and is easy to configure through the app settings.
