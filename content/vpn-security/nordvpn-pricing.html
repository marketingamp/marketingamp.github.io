NordVPN Pricing 2026: Every Plan Compared (Is It Worth It?)

Updated May 21, 2026 • 14 min read

NordVPN is one of the most popular VPNs on the planet, but its pricing page can be genuinely confusing. Three plan tiers, three billing cycles, introductory rates that jump at renewal, and add-ons that may or may not be bundled — it's a lot to sort through.

We've spent weeks digging into every NordVPN plan so you don't have to. In this guide, we break down exactly what each tier costs, what you get (and don't get), and which plan actually makes sense for your situation. If you want the full feature review, check out our NordVPN Review 2026. This article is purely about the money.

Quick Overview: NordVPN Plans at a Glance

NordVPN currently offers three plan tiers — Basic, Plus, and Complete — each available on monthly, 1-year, or 2-year billing cycles. The core VPN is identical across all three tiers. The difference comes down to bundled extras like NordPass (password manager) and NordLocker (encrypted cloud storage).

Here's the short version before we get into the details:

  • Basic — VPN only. Best for users who just want online privacy and don't need extra tools.
  • Plus — VPN + NordPass (password manager). Best value for most people.
  • Complete — VPN + NordPass + NordLocker (1 TB encrypted cloud storage). Best if you want an all-in-one security suite.

All plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee, so there's no risk in trying any tier.

Plan-by-Plan Breakdown

NordVPN Basic: Just the VPN

The Basic plan is pure VPN. You get everything that makes NordVPN one of our top-rated VPNs:

  • 6,400+ servers across 111 countries
  • AES-256 encryption with NordLynx (WireGuard-based) protocol
  • Threat Protection Lite — blocks ads and malicious websites while connected
  • Double VPN for extra encryption layers
  • Onion Over VPN for Tor integration
  • Meshnet — peer-to-peer private networking between your devices
  • 10 simultaneous connections
  • Kill switch and split tunneling
  • No-logs policy (audited by Deloitte)

At $3.09/month on the 2-year plan, this is genuinely cheap for a tier-one VPN. If you already have a password manager and cloud storage you're happy with, Basic is all you need.

NordVPN Plus: The Sweet Spot

Plus adds NordPass, Nord Security's cross-platform password manager. You get everything in Basic, plus:

  • NordPass Premium — unlimited passwords, passkeys, credit card storage, and secure notes
  • Data Breach Scanner — alerts you if your credentials appear in known breaches
  • Password health reports — flags weak, reused, or old passwords
  • Cross-device sync across unlimited devices
  • Full Threat Protection — works even when the VPN is off, scans downloads for malware

NordPass on its own runs about $2.99/month on an annual plan. The Plus tier bundles it for roughly $0.90/month more than Basic on the 2-year cycle. That's a significant saving if you were going to buy a password manager anyway — and most people should be using one.

We think Plus is the best value tier for most users. A VPN and password manager cover the two biggest gaps in most people's personal security setup. We go deeper on this in our Is NordVPN Worth It? breakdown.

NordVPN Complete: The Full Suite

Complete gives you everything in Plus, with the addition of NordLocker:

  • 1 TB of encrypted cloud storage — end-to-end encrypted, zero-knowledge
  • Local file encryption — encrypt files on your device, not just in the cloud
  • File sharing with other NordLocker users

At $5.49/month on the 2-year plan, you're paying roughly $1.50/month more than Plus for a full terabyte of E2E-encrypted storage. For context, iCloud+ 2TB costs $9.99/month and Google One 2TB costs $9.99/month — though neither offers the same level of zero-knowledge encryption.

Complete makes sense if you handle sensitive files regularly (freelancers, consultants, anyone dealing with client data) or if you simply want one security subscription that covers everything.

Which NordVPN Plan Is Best for You?

Here's our take on who should pick which tier:

Choose Basic if...

  • You only need a VPN for streaming, torrenting, or general privacy
  • You already use a password manager you're happy with (1Password, Bitwarden, etc.)
  • You want the absolute lowest price and don't care about extras

Choose Plus if...

  • You don't have a password manager yet (or you're using a bad one)
  • You want the best bang-for-buck security bundle
  • You value the full Threat Protection that scans downloads for malware

Choose Complete if...

  • You need encrypted cloud storage for work or personal files
  • You want a single subscription for VPN + passwords + storage
  • You handle sensitive client data and need zero-knowledge encryption

For most readers of ToolStackReview, we recommend Plus on the 2-year plan. It's the tier we use ourselves.

Hidden Costs and Gotchas

NordVPN's pricing page is straightforward compared to some competitors, but there are a few things to watch for:

1. Renewal Pricing Is Higher

This is the biggest one. The prices shown above (and on NordVPN's website) are introductory rates. When your plan renews, the price increases. For example, the Basic 2-year plan goes from roughly $3.09/month to around $4.59/month at renewal. That's still competitive, but it's worth knowing upfront.

Our tip: You can often find a renewal discount by contacting NordVPN's support chat before your plan expires. We've seen users get 40-50% off renewal rates just by asking.

2. Dedicated IP Costs Extra

If you need a dedicated (static) IP address — useful for accessing IP-restricted networks or avoiding CAPTCHAs — it's an add-on at roughly $3.69/month. This isn't included in any tier.

3. The Monthly Plan Is a Bad Deal

We'll say it plainly: the monthly billing option exists, but it's overpriced. At $12.99-$15.49/month, you're paying premium rates for a service that costs a fraction of that on longer commitments. The 30-day money-back guarantee on annual and 2-year plans makes the monthly plan almost entirely unnecessary.

4. Simultaneous Connections

All plans support 10 simultaneous device connections. That's generous — enough for a household. But if you need more, you'll need to set up NordVPN on your router (which counts as one connection).

5. Tax May Apply

Depending on your country, VAT or sales tax may be added at checkout. The listed prices are pre-tax.

Money-Saving Tips

We've tracked NordVPN's pricing for a while now. Here's how to get the best deal:

  1. Always pick the 2-year plan. The per-month savings over the 1-year plan are substantial, and the 30-day guarantee removes the risk.
  2. Watch for seasonal sales. NordVPN runs promotions during Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and occasionally around holidays. Discounts can reach 65-70% off.
  3. Negotiate at renewal. Before your plan auto-renews, open a support chat and ask about a renewal discount. It works more often than you'd think.
  4. Bundle instead of buying separately. If you need a password manager, the Plus plan is cheaper than buying Basic + a standalone NordPass subscription.
  5. Use the money-back guarantee as a trial. NordVPN doesn't offer a free trial, but the 30-day refund policy is hassle-free. We've tested it ourselves — the refund came through in 5 business days.

Is NordVPN Worth the Price?

Short answer: yes, for most people.

At $3-4/month on a 2-year plan, NordVPN costs less than a single coffee per month. For that, you get military-grade encryption, access to 6,400+ servers in 111 countries, reliable streaming unblocking, a strict no-logs policy, and features like Meshnet and Threat Protection that you won't find on cheaper VPNs.

The Plus plan is the standout value. Adding a full-featured password manager for less than a dollar more per month is a genuinely smart bundle. If you're currently paying for a separate password manager, switching to NordVPN Plus could actually save you money overall.

Where NordVPN isn't worth it:

  • If you only need a VPN for a month or two — the monthly pricing isn't competitive
  • If you need unlimited simultaneous connections — Surfshark is the better pick
  • If you're on a very tight budget — some VPNs like CyberGhost and Surfshark undercut NordVPN on sticker price

But for the vast majority of users who want a reliable, fast, and well-featured VPN at a fair price, NordVPN delivers. We cover the full argument in our Is NordVPN Worth It? article.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest NordVPN plan in 2026?

The cheapest option is the Basic 2-year plan at around $3.09/month (billed as a lump sum of $83.43). This gets you the full VPN with all core features, just without the password manager and encrypted storage add-ons.

Is NordVPN Plus worth the upgrade over Basic?

If you don't already have a password manager, absolutely. NordPass alone costs about $2.99/month. Bundling it via the Plus plan adds roughly $0.90/month to your VPN cost — a clear saving. If you already use 1Password, Bitwarden, or another manager, stick with Basic.

Does NordVPN offer a free trial or money-back guarantee?

No free trial, but every plan comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. This works as a de facto trial. We tested the refund process and received our money back within 5 business days, no questions asked. If you want to learn more about NordVPN's privacy credentials before committing, see our Best VPN for Privacy guide.

Final Verdict

NordVPN's pricing is competitive, especially on the 2-year plans. The three-tier structure (Basic, Plus, Complete) is well thought out — each tier adds meaningful value rather than just padding the feature list.

Our recommendation for most readers: go with the Plus 2-year plan. At roughly $3.99/month, you get a top-tier VPN and a solid password manager in a single subscription. That's hard to beat.

If you want the absolute lowest cost and already have your password situation sorted, the Basic 2-year plan at $3.09/month is excellent. And if you need encrypted cloud storage, the Complete plan is actually well-priced compared to buying everything separately.

Whatever tier you choose, commit to at least a 1-year plan. The monthly pricing isn't worth it when the 30-day money-back guarantee eliminates all risk on longer commitments.
