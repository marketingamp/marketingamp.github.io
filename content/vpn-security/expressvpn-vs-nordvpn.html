ExpressVPN vs NordVPN 2026: Which VPN Wins?

Updated May 12, 2026 • 12 min read

ExpressVPN and NordVPN are the two most recognized VPN brands in the world, and for good reason. Both deliver strong security, fast speeds, and reliable streaming access. But they take different approaches to get there, and the right choice depends on what you value most.

We tested both VPNs side by side for four weeks, measuring speed across five server locations, testing streaming services, auditing security features, and evaluating the apps on every major platform. Here's how they compare.

Speed

Both VPNs are fast, but ExpressVPN has a slight edge. Using the Lightway protocol, ExpressVPN retained 95% of our base connection speed on nearby servers and 88% on long-distance connections (US to Japan). NordVPN's NordLynx protocol delivered 93% on nearby servers and 85% on long-distance routes.

In practical terms, both are fast enough for 4K streaming, large file downloads, and video conferencing without noticeable degradation. The 2% difference only becomes meaningful if you're on a very fast connection (500+ Mbps) where every percentage point translates to real throughput.

Winner: ExpressVPN by a narrow margin. Both are among the fastest VPNs available.

Security and Privacy

Both providers use AES-256 encryption, operate RAM-only server infrastructure (no data written to disk), and have verified no-logs policies backed by independent audits. Both are headquartered in privacy-friendly jurisdictions — ExpressVPN in the British Virgin Islands, NordVPN in Panama.

Where NordVPN pulls ahead is in additional security features. Threat Protection blocks malicious websites, ads, and trackers at the network level — similar to what you'd get from a separate tool like Pi-hole or uBlock Origin. NordVPN also offers Double VPN (routing traffic through two servers), Onion over VPN (connecting to the Tor network), and Meshnet (creating encrypted private networks between devices). ExpressVPN has none of these extras.

ExpressVPN's advantage is its Lightway protocol, which is open-source and has been independently audited by Cure53. NordLynx is based on the open-source WireGuard protocol but includes a proprietary double NAT system that hasn't received the same level of public scrutiny.

Winner: NordVPN. The additional security tools (Threat Protection, Double VPN, Meshnet) provide tangible value that ExpressVPN doesn't match.

Streaming

Both VPNs reliably unblock major streaming services. In our testing, ExpressVPN and NordVPN both successfully accessed Netflix (US, UK, Japan, Germany, Australia), Disney+, BBC iPlayer, Amazon Prime Video, HBO Max, and Hulu. Neither failed during our four-week test period.

ExpressVPN has a slight advantage in consistency — it connected to every streaming service on the first attempt, every time. NordVPN occasionally required switching servers to find one that wasn't blocked, particularly for Netflix Japan and BBC iPlayer. The difference is minor but noticeable if you stream frequently from multiple regions.

Both providers offer smart DNS features (ExpressVPN's MediaStreamer and NordVPN's SmartPlay) that enable streaming on devices that don't support VPN apps natively, like smart TVs and gaming consoles.

Winner: ExpressVPN by a slim margin for first-attempt reliability. Both unblock the same services.

Apps and Ease of Use

ExpressVPN's apps are the most polished in the VPN industry. The interface is identical across Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, and Linux — a one-click connect button with a simple server list. There's no clutter, no confusing settings menus, and the app connects in under two seconds on Lightway. If you want a VPN that just works without thinking about it, ExpressVPN sets the standard.

NordVPN's apps are more feature-rich but slightly more complex. The main screen shows an interactive server map (which some users love and others find unnecessary), and there are more settings to configure — Threat Protection, split tunneling, kill switch options, protocol selection, and specialty server types. The trade-off is that NordVPN's apps can feel busier, especially for non-technical users.

Winner: ExpressVPN for simplicity. NordVPN for power users who want more control.

NordVPN also includes more features (Threat Protection, Meshnet) at that lower price.

Winner: NordVPN. Significantly cheaper on long-term plans while offering more features.

Server Network

NordVPN operates over 6,400 servers across 111 countries. ExpressVPN runs 3,000+ servers across 105 countries. Both networks cover every major region, but NordVPN's larger fleet means less congestion during peak hours and more options for finding a fast server in any given country.

NordVPN also offers specialty servers — Double VPN, Onion over VPN, P2P-optimized, and obfuscated servers for restrictive networks. ExpressVPN treats all servers as equal-purpose, which simplifies the experience but removes the option to optimize for specific use cases.

Winner: NordVPN. More servers, more countries, and specialty options.

Which Should You Choose?

Our Verdict

Choose ExpressVPN if: You want the fastest speeds, the most polished apps, and a VPN that just works without configuration. ExpressVPN is the better choice for non-technical users, streaming enthusiasts who want first-attempt reliability, and anyone willing to pay a premium for the smoothest experience.

Choose NordVPN if: You want the best value, the most features, and a VPN that goes beyond basic encryption. Threat Protection, Double VPN, Meshnet, and a larger server network make NordVPN the more complete package. At roughly half the price of ExpressVPN on long-term plans, it's the smarter investment for most people.

Bottom line: NordVPN wins overall. It matches ExpressVPN on the fundamentals (speed, security, streaming) while offering more features at a lower price. ExpressVPN is worth the premium only if app simplicity and raw speed are your top priorities.
