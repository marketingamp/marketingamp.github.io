Is NordVPN Worth It in 2026? Honest Answer After 6 Months

NordVPN is the most popular VPN in the world, but popular does not always mean worth your money. After using it as my primary VPN for six months — for work, streaming, and travel — here is my straightforward take on whether it is actually worth paying for.

The Short Answer

Yes, NordVPN is worth it for most people. It is not the cheapest VPN (Surfshark and Proton VPN cost less), but the combination of speed, security features, and streaming reliability makes it the best overall package. If you only need a VPN occasionally, a cheaper option may be fine. If you want something you can trust daily without thinking about it, NordVPN delivers.

What You Get for $3.59/Month

On the 2-year Standard plan ($3.59/month), NordVPN includes 6,400+ servers in 111 countries, the NordLynx protocol (based on WireGuard, extremely fast), a verified no-logs policy audited by Deloitte, Threat Protection (blocks ads, trackers, and malware), Meshnet (secure remote access to your own devices), and 10 simultaneous device connections.

The Plus plan ($4.59/month) adds NordPass password manager and Data Breach Scanner. The Complete plan ($5.59/month) adds 1TB of NordLocker encrypted cloud storage.

Where NordVPN Earns Its Price

Speed: NordLynx is consistently the fastest VPN protocol I have tested. Connecting to a nearby server, I lose less than 5% of my base speed. Even connecting to servers across the Atlantic, streaming and video calls work without hiccups.

Streaming: This is where NordVPN separates from cheaper competitors. It reliably unblocks Netflix US, UK, and Japan, Disney+, BBC iPlayer, Hulu, and Amazon Prime Video. Surfshark and Proton VPN occasionally fail on some services; NordVPN has not failed me once in six months.

Threat Protection: This feature alone is worth something. It blocks malicious websites, ads, and web trackers at the system level — not just in your browser. It works even when the VPN is disconnected, which makes it a lightweight security layer on top of everything else.

Reliability: I have never had the VPN drop during a call, fail to connect, or need troubleshooting. It works. That sounds basic, but after trying VPNs that require regular app restarts or server switching, reliability matters more than any feature list.

Where NordVPN Falls Short

Price: At $3.59/month (2-year commitment), it is not expensive in absolute terms, but Surfshark offers unlimited devices for $2.29/month and Proton VPN has a genuine free tier. If budget is your primary concern, those are real alternatives.

No split tunneling on Mac/iOS: You cannot route some apps through the VPN and others outside it on Apple devices. This feature exists on Windows and Android. If you need this on a Mac, Surfshark or ExpressVPN handle it better.

The 2-year commitment: The low price requires paying upfront for two years. The monthly plan is $12.99/month — steep for occasional use. The 30-day money-back guarantee mitigates the risk, but you are still locking in.

NordVPN is worth it if you value reliability, speed, and hassle-free streaming access. It is the VPN I would recommend to someone who just wants the best option without researching every alternative. If you are on a tight budget and don't care about streaming, Surfshark or Proton VPN's free tier are solid alternatives.
