GetResponse offers more than just email — landing pages, webinars, and automation are included. Here's what each plan costs and which one you actually need.

Free Plan — $0/month

$0/month

GetResponse's free plan covers 500 contacts with 2,500 email sends per month. It's more limited than Mailchimp's free tier in volume but includes a landing page builder and website builder.

  • 500 contacts, 2,500 emails/month
  • Email creator with templates
  • 1 landing page (1,000 visits/mo)
  • Website builder
  • Signup forms and popups
  • No credit card required

Best for: Testing the platform before committing. Good for personal projects or very early-stage businesses.

Email Marketing — Starting at $19/month

$19/month (1,000 contacts)

The Email Marketing plan is the core offering. Unlimited email sends, autoresponders, A/B testing, and basic segmentation. It's competitive with Mailchimp's Standard plan but generally cheaper at higher contact counts.

  • Unlimited email sends
  • AI email generator
  • Autoresponders
  • Unlimited landing pages
  • Website builder
  • Basic segmentation
  • A/B testing (subject lines)
  • Google & Meta ads integration

Best for: Small businesses focused on email newsletters, basic automations, and landing pages. Covers 80% of what most email marketers need.

Marketing Automation — Starting at $59/month

$59/month (1,000 contacts)

Marketing Automation adds visual workflow builders, advanced segmentation, event-based triggers, and webinars. This is where GetResponse differentiates from basic email tools.

  • Everything in Email Marketing, plus:
  • Visual automation builder
  • Event-based workflows
  • Advanced segmentation
  • Contact scoring
  • Webinars (up to 100 attendees)
  • Sales funnels
  • 3 team members

Best for: Growing businesses that need behavior-based automations, lead scoring, and webinars as part of their funnel.

E-commerce Marketing — Starting at $119/month

$119/month (1,000 contacts)

The E-commerce tier adds transactional emails, abandoned cart recovery, product recommendations, and promo codes. Built specifically for online stores running on Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento.

  • Everything in Marketing Automation, plus:
  • Transactional emails
  • Abandoned cart recovery
  • Product recommendations
  • Promo codes
  • Web push notifications
  • E-commerce segmentation
  • 5 team members

Best for: E-commerce stores that want email, automation, and transactional messaging in one platform instead of paying separately for Klaviyo ($150+/mo).

Which Plan Should You Choose?

Start with Free if: You have under 500 contacts and want to test the platform. Upgrade when you hit the limit.

Choose Email Marketing if: You mainly send newsletters and simple autoresponders. At $19/mo for 1,000 contacts with unlimited sends, it's great value.

Choose Marketing Automation if: You need behavior-based workflows, lead scoring, or webinars. The visual automation builder alone is worth the upgrade.

Choose E-commerce if: You run an online store and want abandoned cart recovery + product recommendations without paying Klaviyo prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does GetResponse cost?

GetResponse starts at $19/month for 1,000 contacts on the Email Marketing plan. Pricing scales with list size — 5,000 contacts costs $54/mo, 10,000 costs $79/mo. Annual billing saves 18%.

Does GetResponse have a free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes 500 contacts, 2,500 emails/month, 1 landing page, and a website builder. No credit card required and no time limit.

Is GetResponse cheaper than Mailchimp?

Generally yes, especially at higher subscriber counts. At 10,000 contacts, GetResponse is $79/mo vs Mailchimp's ~$100/mo. Plus GetResponse includes landing pages and webinars that Mailchimp doesn't offer.
