GetResponse Review 2026: Email Marketing, Automation & More

Updated May 15, 2026 • 13 min read

GetResponse has been in the email marketing space for over 25 years, but it's quietly evolved into something much bigger than a simple email tool. Today it's a full marketing platform with email, automation, landing pages, webinars, conversion funnels, e-commerce tools, and AI-powered content generation — all under one roof.

We tested GetResponse for three months across a real email list of 5,000+ subscribers, running campaigns, building automations, and testing every major feature. Here's what we found.

GetResponse offers 18% off for annual plans and 30% off for two-year plans, which brings the Email Marketing plan down to as low as $13.30/month for 1,000 contacts. That significantly undercuts most competitors.

Email Marketing

The core email marketing is excellent. GetResponse's drag-and-drop email editor is one of the most polished in the industry, with a large template library (100+ designs), dynamic content blocks, mobile preview, and inbox rendering tests. You can build professional-looking emails quickly without touching HTML.

Key email features include A/B testing (subject lines, content, send times), send-time optimization (AI analyzes when each subscriber is most likely to open), RSS-to-email for automated blog digests, and AMP email support for interactive content. The AI email generator can draft email copy from a prompt, which saves significant time for routine campaigns.

Deliverability is strong. GetResponse maintains a dedicated compliance team and provides built-in spam scoring before you send. In our testing, inbox placement rates averaged 89% across Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo — above the industry average of about 83%.

Marketing Automation

GetResponse's automation builder is where it starts to separate from basic email tools like Mailchimp or MailerLite. The visual workflow builder lets you create multi-step automations triggered by subscriber actions: email opens, clicks, purchases, page visits, tag changes, custom events, and more.

You can build sophisticated sequences like: abandoned cart recovery workflows, lead scoring systems that tag hot prospects, webinar registration and follow-up sequences, post-purchase onboarding drips, and win-back campaigns for inactive subscribers. The automation is available starting on the Marketing Automation plan ($59/month for 1,000 contacts).

Compared to ActiveCampaign (the automation benchmark), GetResponse's automation is slightly less flexible in terms of branching logic and conditional complexity, but it covers 90% of what most small businesses need at a lower price point.

Built-In Webinars

GetResponse is the only major email marketing platform with built-in webinar hosting. You can host live webinars for up to 1,000 attendees (depending on plan), with screen sharing, polls, chat, file sharing, call-to-action buttons, and recording. Webinar registrants are automatically added to your email list with proper tags, making follow-up seamless.

This is a significant differentiator. If webinars are part of your marketing strategy, using GetResponse eliminates the need for a separate webinar platform ($49-$199/month saved), and the integration between webinar attendance data and email automation is seamless.

Landing Pages and Conversion Funnels

GetResponse includes a landing page builder with 200+ templates, A/B testing, and countdown timers. The landing pages connect directly to your email lists, so you don't need separate form tools or integrations.

The Conversion Funnel feature (formerly Autofunnel) takes this further by creating complete sales funnels: landing page, email sequence, webinar, sales page, and checkout. It's a lighter version of what dedicated funnel builders offer, but it's included in your email marketing subscription — no additional cost.

AI Features

GetResponse has integrated AI across the platform: AI email generator (draft emails from a prompt), AI subject line generator, AI landing page creator, and AI campaign builder that creates a complete campaign from a description of your goal. The AI tools use GPT-4 under the hood and produce surprisingly usable first drafts.

The AI campaign builder is particularly impressive — describe your goal (e.g., "promote my new online course to existing subscribers"), and it generates a complete campaign with email copy, subject lines, recommended send times, and landing page suggestions.

E-commerce Integration

GetResponse integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop, and custom stores via API. The Ecommerce Marketing plan adds transactional emails, product recommendations based on purchase history, abandoned cart emails, promo code management, and advanced segmentation based on purchase behavior.

For e-commerce businesses, these features compete with dedicated tools like Klaviyo at a fraction of the price (GetResponse's Ecommerce plan is $119/month for 1,000 contacts vs Klaviyo's ~$45/month for 1,000 contacts, but GetResponse includes landing pages, webinars, and funnels).

Who Is GetResponse Best For?

Our Verdict

Ideal for: Small to mid-size businesses that want more than just email marketing but don't want to pay for separate webinar, landing page, and funnel tools. GetResponse is especially strong for businesses that use webinars in their marketing strategy, since it's the only email platform with built-in webinar hosting. Also excellent for e-commerce businesses needing product recommendations and abandoned cart recovery.

Not ideal for: Businesses that only need basic email newsletters (MailerLite is cheaper), large enterprises needing complex CRM integration (HubSpot is better), or creators who prioritize simplicity over features (Kit is more focused).

Our take: GetResponse offers the best feature-to-price ratio in email marketing for 2026. The combination of solid email marketing, visual automation, built-in webinars, landing pages, and conversion funnels — all starting at $19/month — is hard to beat. The free plan is a legitimate way to test everything before committing. If you're outgrowing basic email tools and want a platform that can grow with you, GetResponse is a smart choice.
