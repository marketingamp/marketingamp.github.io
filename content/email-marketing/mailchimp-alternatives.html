8 Best Mailchimp Alternatives (Better Features, Lower Prices) 2026

Updated May 16, 2026 • 18 min read

Mailchimp used to be the default email marketing tool. Free plan up to 2,000 subscribers, easy drag-and-drop builder, good enough automation. Then Intuit bought it, and things changed. The free plan shrank to 500 contacts and 1,000 sends per month. Prices climbed. Features got locked behind higher tiers. Automation — once free — now requires the Standard plan at $20/month for just 500 contacts.

Meanwhile, competitors have gotten better and cheaper. Several now offer more generous free plans, better automation, higher deliverability, and lower prices at every tier. If you're paying Mailchimp prices in 2026, you're likely overpaying for what you get.

We migrated a 5,000-subscriber list to each of these eight platforms and tested deliverability, automation capabilities, template quality, and total cost. Here's what we found.

1. Kit (ConvertKit) — Best for Creators & Newsletter Writers

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) was built from the ground up for creators: bloggers, YouTubers, podcasters, authors, and anyone who makes a living from their audience. Its free plan supports up to 10,000 subscribers — twenty times what Mailchimp offers — which alone makes it worth considering.

What sets Kit apart is its approach to subscriber management. Instead of Mailchimp's rigid list-based system (where the same person on two lists counts as two contacts), Kit uses tags and segments across a single subscriber pool. Every subscriber is one record, no matter how many tags you apply. This eliminates duplicate charges and makes segmentation far more flexible.

The visual automation builder is clean and powerful. You can build complex sequences with conditional logic, delays, and branching paths — all on the free plan. Kit also offers paid newsletter subscriptions and a tip jar feature, letting your audience pay you directly through the platform.

For affiliate marketers specifically, Kit's automation workflows are ideal for welcome sequences that recommend tools and earn commissions on autopilot. Its 30% recurring commission for 24 months makes it one of the better affiliate programs in the email space.

Pros

  • Free plan supports 10,000 subscribers (vs Mailchimp's 500)
  • Tag-based system eliminates duplicate subscriber charges
  • Visual automation builder on all plans including free
  • Paid newsletter and tip jar features for creators
  • 30% recurring affiliate commission for 24 months

Cons

  • Email templates are intentionally minimal (plain-text focused)
  • No built-in CRM or sales pipeline
  • Reporting is basic compared to ActiveCampaign

2. MailerLite — Best Free Mailchimp Alternative

MailerLite offers the best overall free plan for people who just need to send good-looking emails with basic automation. The free tier includes 1,000 subscribers, 12,000 monthly emails, a drag-and-drop editor, 10 landing pages, signup forms, and — critically — automation workflows. Mailchimp locks automation behind its $20/month Standard plan.

The paid plans start at just $9/month for up to 500 subscribers with unlimited emails, advanced automations, A/B testing, and the ability to remove MailerLite branding. At 5,000 subscribers, you'd pay $32/month with MailerLite vs. $75/month with Mailchimp — a 57% savings.

MailerLite's email editor is genuinely excellent. The drag-and-drop builder is as smooth as Mailchimp's (which set the standard), and the template library is clean and modern. The built-in website builder and blog are bonuses that newer competitors don't always include.

Pros

  • Free plan includes automation (Mailchimp doesn't)
  • Paid plans 50-60% cheaper than Mailchimp at every tier
  • Excellent drag-and-drop editor rivaling Mailchimp's
  • Built-in website builder and blog on paid plans

Cons

  • Approval process can delay account activation
  • Free plan limited to 1,000 subs (Kit offers 10,000)
  • Fewer integrations than Mailchimp or ActiveCampaign

3. Brevo (Sendinblue) — Best for Unlimited Contacts

Brevo has a pricing model that flips Mailchimp's approach on its head. Instead of charging by number of contacts (where Mailchimp penalizes you for growing your list), Brevo charges by number of emails sent. You can have unlimited contacts on every plan, including the free tier.

The free plan allows 300 emails per day (about 9,000/month) to unlimited contacts. The Starter plan at $9/month bumps that to 5,000 emails/month. For businesses with large lists they email infrequently — like monthly newsletters to a big subscriber base — Brevo can be dramatically cheaper than Mailchimp.

Beyond email, Brevo includes SMS marketing, WhatsApp campaigns, live chat, and a basic CRM on all plans. It's positioned as a complete customer communication platform, not just an email tool. The transactional email API is also excellent, making Brevo a strong choice if you need both marketing and transactional emails from one provider.

Pros

  • Unlimited contacts on all plans (including free)
  • SMS, WhatsApp, and live chat included
  • Strong transactional email API
  • Built-in CRM at no extra cost

Cons

  • Free plan limited to 300 emails/day
  • Email editor is functional but not as polished as MailerLite
  • Advanced automation requires the Business plan ($18/mo)

4. ActiveCampaign — Best Automation for Power Users

If email automation is your primary need, ActiveCampaign is the gold standard. Its visual automation builder is the most powerful on this list — conditional splits, wait conditions, goal tracking, lead scoring, site tracking, and predictive sending are all available. No other tool on this list matches ActiveCampaign's automation depth.

ActiveCampaign also includes a built-in CRM on the Plus plan ($49/mo), making it a genuine marketing and sales platform. The CRM ties directly into your email automations, so you can trigger emails based on deal stage changes, assign leads to sales reps based on behavior, and track the full customer journey from first email to closed deal.

At $15/month for 1,000 contacts, ActiveCampaign is priced competitively with Mailchimp's Standard plan but delivers significantly more automation power. The trade-off is complexity — there's a real learning curve, and the interface can feel overwhelming if you just want to send a newsletter.

5. GetResponse — Best All-in-One Marketing Platform

GetResponse has quietly evolved from an email tool into a full marketing platform. Beyond email, it includes landing pages, webinars (up to 1,000 attendees), conversion funnels, website builder, and even a course creator. If you want to replace multiple tools with one platform, GetResponse packs more features per dollar than almost any competitor.

The webinar feature is the standout differentiator. No other email marketing platform includes built-in webinar hosting. If webinars are part of your marketing strategy, GetResponse eliminates the need for a separate tool like Zoom or WebinarJam — saving $50–$100/month.

Email deliverability is strong. GetResponse consistently ranks in the top tier of deliverability tests, and the automation builder has improved significantly in recent updates. At $15.60/month for 1,000 contacts, it's priced similarly to Mailchimp's Standard plan but includes far more features.

6. AWeber — Best Deliverability for Small Business

AWeber has been in the email marketing game since 1998 — longer than any other tool on this list. That longevity translates to established relationships with ISPs and consistently strong deliverability. If your emails landing in the inbox (rather than spam) is your top priority, AWeber's track record is hard to beat.

The free plan covers 500 subscribers with access to most features including automation, landing pages, and web push notifications. The paid plan at $15/month removes AWeber branding and adds advanced features like split testing and behavioral automation.

AWeber's template library is one of the largest available, with 700+ pre-designed templates. The Canva integration lets you design email graphics without leaving the platform. For small businesses that want reliable email delivery without complexity, AWeber is a solid, no-drama choice.

7. Constant Contact — Best for Events & Nonprofits

Constant Contact is the best email platform if events are a core part of your business. Its built-in event management features — registration pages, invitations, RSVP tracking, and ticket sales — are unique among email marketing tools. No other platform on this list offers anything comparable.

For nonprofits, Constant Contact offers discounted pricing and donation collection tools built right into email campaigns. The social media posting and ad management features are also more robust than most email tools, making it a genuine multi-channel marketing platform for organizations that need simplicity.

Starting at $12/month, Constant Contact is priced competitively. The 60-day free trial is the longest in the industry, giving you two full months to evaluate before paying. The trade-off is automation — Constant Contact's automation is significantly simpler than ActiveCampaign or even Kit.

8. Omnisend — Best for E-commerce

If you run a Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce store, Omnisend is purpose-built for you. Its pre-built automation workflows cover the e-commerce essentials — welcome series, abandoned cart recovery, order confirmation, shipping updates, product recommendations, and win-back campaigns — with templates optimized for each scenario.

Omnisend's product picker lets you drag products directly from your store into emails, automatically pulling in images, prices, and descriptions. The segmentation is e-commerce-native: filter by purchase history, cart value, product browsing behavior, and customer lifetime value. These are features Mailchimp can approximate with integrations, but Omnisend has them built in.

The free plan supports 250 contacts and 500 emails/month with access to all features. Paid plans start at $16/month for 500 contacts. For dedicated e-commerce email marketing, Omnisend delivers more relevant features at a lower price than Mailchimp plus a Shopify integration.

Which Mailchimp Alternative Should You Choose?

Our Verdict

For creators and newsletters: Kit (ConvertKit) with its 10,000-subscriber free plan and creator-focused features is the clear winner. Tag-based management alone is worth the switch from Mailchimp's list system.

For the best free plan: MailerLite gives you automation, landing pages, and a polished editor for free. It's the closest thing to "old Mailchimp" before the Intuit acquisition.

For large lists on a budget: Brevo's unlimited contacts model means you never pay more as your list grows. Ideal for infrequent senders with big lists.

For advanced automation: ActiveCampaign is the powerhouse. If automation drives your revenue, its depth justifies the price.

For e-commerce: Omnisend with its native Shopify/WooCommerce integration and pre-built e-commerce automations beats Mailchimp's generic approach.

How We Tested

We migrated a real 5,000-subscriber email list to each platform and sent identical campaigns over 30 days. We measured inbox placement rate across Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo; tracked open rates and click rates on the same content; tested automation builder complexity; and compared template quality and editor usability. Pricing was calculated at the 1,000, 5,000, and 25,000 subscriber tiers.
