5 Best Free Landing Page Builders in 2026 (No Credit Card)

Updated May 12, 2026 • 10 min read

Not every landing page needs a $99/month tool behind it. If you're validating an idea, collecting emails for a side project, or running a small campaign on a tight budget, a free landing page builder can do the job without asking for your credit card.

We signed up for five genuinely free landing page tools and built the same lead-capture page on each: a headline, subheadline, email form, and call-to-action button. Here's how they compare on design flexibility, limitations, and what you actually get for free.

1. Carrd — Best for Simple One-Page Sites

Carrd is the fastest way to go from zero to a published landing page. The free plan gives you three sites on a carrd.co subdomain with access to a curated set of templates. The builder is minimal by design — you drag sections into a single-page layout and customize text, colors, images, and buttons. There are no multi-page sites, no pop-ups, and no complex logic. That's the point.

For a link-in-bio page, a coming-soon page, or a simple email capture form, Carrd is unbeatable. You can have a live page in under ten minutes. The free plan does have limits: no custom domain, no embedded forms from third-party tools, and no Google Analytics. Upgrading to Carrd Pro ($19/year) unlocks all of that and remains one of the cheapest paid options anywhere.

2. Mailchimp Landing Pages — Best for Email List Building

If your goal is collecting email addresses, Mailchimp's free landing pages are hard to ignore. They're bundled with Mailchimp's free email marketing plan (up to 500 contacts), so you get a landing page builder and an email platform in one signup. Pages connect directly to your Mailchimp audience, meaning every form submission flows straight into your email list with no extra integration steps.

The builder is straightforward. You pick from a handful of templates, customize the layout with a drag-and-drop editor, and publish to a Mailchimp subdomain. Design options are limited compared to dedicated landing page tools, but for a free product, the integration with Mailchimp's email automation is the real draw.

3. Google Sites — Best for Quick, No-Fuss Pages

Google Sites is completely free with any Google account. There are no subscriber limits, no page limits, and you can even connect a custom domain through Google Workspace. The builder feels like a simplified Google Docs — you type content, insert images from Google Drive, embed YouTube videos, and arrange sections in a column layout.

The downside is obvious: Google Sites was not built for conversion. There are no built-in forms optimized for lead capture (you embed Google Forms instead), no A/B testing, no analytics beyond basic page views, and limited design customization. It works for internal team pages, event signups, or quick-launch projects where aesthetics aren't the priority.

4. HubSpot Free Landing Pages — Best for Lead Gen with CRM

HubSpot's free tier includes up to 20 landing pages with a drag-and-drop builder, form capture, and direct integration with HubSpot's free CRM. Every lead that fills out your form automatically appears as a contact in your CRM with a tracked activity timeline. That's a powerful workflow for free.

The builder itself is functional but not flashy. Templates are limited on the free plan, and pages display HubSpot branding. Design flexibility is moderate — you can customize colors, images, and text, but you're working within a structured grid. Where HubSpot shines is the ecosystem: leads flow into contact records, email sequences, and deal pipelines without any third-party tools.

5. ConvertKit Free Landing Pages — Best for Creators

ConvertKit (now Kit) offers a free plan that includes one landing page and unlimited email subscribers with basic email sends. The landing page builder is template-driven — you pick from a set of clean, creator-focused designs and customize text, images, and colors. Options are intentionally limited to keep pages looking polished.

ConvertKit's free landing pages are purpose-built for newsletter signups, freebie downloads, and waitlists. If you're a creator growing an audience, the tight integration between the landing page and ConvertKit's subscriber tagging and email sequences makes this a natural choice. The limitation is that you get only one landing page on the free plan.

Which Free Builder Should You Choose?

Our Verdict

For the fastest launch: Carrd. You can have a live landing page in minutes. If you outgrow the free plan, Pro is just $19/year — the best value upgrade on this list.

For email list building: Mailchimp. Getting landing pages and email marketing in one free platform eliminates the integration headache.

For lead generation with tracking: HubSpot. The free CRM connection means every lead is automatically tracked with full context. No other free tool matches that workflow.

For creators: ConvertKit. If you're building a newsletter or audience, the subscriber management and email automation make it the natural choice.

Bottom line: None of these tools will replace a dedicated landing page builder like Unbounce or Leadpages for high-volume campaigns. But for testing ideas, growing a small list, or launching on zero budget, they do the job well enough that you might never need to upgrade.
