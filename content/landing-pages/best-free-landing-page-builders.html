7 Best Free Landing Page Builders in 2026 (No Credit Card Required)

You do not need to pay $50-100/month for a landing page builder. Several tools offer genuinely useful free plans that let you build, publish, and collect leads without entering a credit card.

I tested every free landing page builder I could find and narrowed it down to seven that actually work — meaning they produce pages that look professional, load quickly, and include the features you need to convert visitors.

1. Systeme.io — Best Overall Free Landing Page Builder

Systeme.io is the most generous free landing page builder available. The free plan includes 3 complete sales funnels (each with multiple pages), built-in email marketing with up to 2,000 contacts, a custom domain, an online course builder, and a blog. No other free tool matches this breadth.

Why it stands out: Unlike tools that just give you a page builder, Systeme.io gives you an entire marketing platform for free. You can build a landing page, connect an email sequence, deliver a digital product, and manage it all from one dashboard. The funnel builder uses a step-by-step interface that makes it easy to create opt-in funnels, sales pages, and thank-you pages.

Free plan includes: 3 sales funnels, 1 email campaign, 2,000 contacts, 1 course with unlimited students, 1 custom domain, blog, and affiliate management.

Limitations: The page editor is simpler than Unbounce or Instapage — fewer templates and less design flexibility. But for landing pages that convert, simplicity is often an advantage.

2. Carrd — Best for Simple One-Page Sites

Carrd is purpose-built for one-page websites and landing pages. The free plan lets you create 3 sites with a carrd.co subdomain. The editor is minimalist and fast — you can have a clean landing page live in under 10 minutes.

Why it stands out: If all you need is a single, focused page (like a link-in-bio, a waitlist, or an event signup), Carrd produces the cleanest results with the least effort. Pages load extremely fast and look polished on mobile.

Limitations: Free sites include a small Carrd badge and cannot use a custom domain. The Pro plan removes branding and adds forms, analytics, and custom domains for $9/year — one of the best deals in web tools.

3. Mailchimp — Best for Email Marketing + Landing Pages

If you are already collecting emails (or plan to), Mailchimp's free plan includes a landing page builder alongside email marketing for up to 500 contacts. The landing page templates are designed specifically for lead capture and are tightly integrated with Mailchimp's email tools.

Why it stands out: The landing page connects directly to your email list with no extra setup. Build a page, publish it, and leads go straight into your Mailchimp audience with tags and segments ready to go.

Limitations: Pages show Mailchimp branding, you cannot use a custom domain on the free plan, and the design flexibility is limited compared to dedicated landing page builders.

4. HubSpot — Best Free CRM + Landing Pages

HubSpot's free CRM includes a landing page builder with up to 20 pages. The templates are professional and the builder integrates with HubSpot's forms, live chat, and contact management — all free.

Why it stands out: If you want landing pages connected to a CRM for lead tracking and follow-up, HubSpot gives you more sales infrastructure than any other free option.

Limitations: HubSpot branding on free pages, limited template customization, and the CRM can feel overwhelming if all you need is a simple landing page.

5. Google Sites — Best for Quick, Simple Pages

Google Sites is completely free with no branding, no limits, and custom domain support. It is the simplest builder on this list — you can create a basic page in under 5 minutes with drag-and-drop sections.

Why it stands out: Zero cost, zero branding, zero learning curve. If you need a simple informational landing page or an internal page for your team, Google Sites is unbeatable for speed.

Limitations: Very limited design options, no built-in forms (you embed Google Forms), no analytics, and pages look generic. Not suitable if conversion optimization matters.

6. Kit (ConvertKit) — Best for Creators and Newsletters

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) offers unlimited landing pages on the free plan, designed specifically for creators who want to grow an email list. Templates are minimal and focused — one clear goal per page.

Why it stands out: The landing pages are purpose-built for email opt-ins and connect directly to Kit's email marketing tools. The free plan includes up to 10,000 subscribers (with limitations on automation), making it one of the most generous email + landing page combos available.

Limitations: Kit branding on free pages, limited design customization, and no e-commerce or funnel features.

7. Elementor — Best for WordPress Users

If you already have a WordPress site, Elementor's free plugin adds a powerful drag-and-drop page builder with landing page templates. No extra subscription needed — install the plugin and start building.

Why it stands out: The most design-flexible free option on this list. Elementor's editor gives you pixel-level control over every element, and because it runs on your own WordPress site, there is no branding and full custom domain support.

Limitations: Requires WordPress hosting (not free unless you use WordPress.com's limited free plan). The free version lacks some templates and widgets that are in Pro ($59/year).

Which Free Builder Should You Choose?

Want the most features for free? Systeme.io — funnels, email, courses, and a custom domain at $0/month.

Need a simple one-page site fast? Carrd — clean, fast, minimal. Upgrade to Pro for $9/year if you want a custom domain.

Already using email marketing? Mailchimp or Kit, depending on whether you prioritize e-commerce (Mailchimp) or creator tools (Kit).

Want CRM integration? HubSpot gives you landing pages plus a full CRM for free.

On WordPress already? Elementor — no extra cost, maximum design control.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I build a professional landing page for free?

Yes. Tools like Systeme.io and Carrd let you build clean, conversion-focused landing pages without paying anything. The main limitations are custom domain support (usually paid) and branding removal. For collecting leads and testing ideas, free plans are more than adequate.

Which free landing page builder has no branding?

Systeme.io's free plan allows you to use a custom domain and has minimal branding. Most other free builders (Mailchimp, HubSpot, Wix) show their branding on free plans. Carrd removes branding on its $9/year Pro plan, which is the cheapest way to get a brandless page.

Do I need a landing page builder or can I use my website builder?

It depends on your goal. If you need a simple page to collect emails or promote an offer, a dedicated landing page builder is faster and often converts better because the templates are designed specifically for conversion. If you already have WordPress or Wix, plugins like Elementor can add landing page functionality without an extra tool.
