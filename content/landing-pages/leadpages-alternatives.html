1. Unbounce — Best for Conversion Optimization

Unbounce pioneered the landing page builder category and remains the gold standard for conversion-focused marketers. Its standout feature is Smart Traffic — an AI that automatically sends visitors to the page variant most likely to convert them based on their attributes.

Pros

  • AI-powered Smart Traffic optimization
  • 100+ high-converting templates
  • Advanced A/B testing built-in
  • Dynamic text replacement for PPC
  • Sticky bars and popups included

Cons

  • More expensive than Leadpages
  • Learning curve for advanced features
  • Visitor limits on lower plans

Pricing: Starts at $74/mo (Build plan). Smart Traffic available on $112/mo plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest Leadpages alternative?

Carrd is the cheapest at $19/year for basic landing pages. For a full-featured free alternative, Systeme.io offers landing pages, email marketing, and funnels on its free plan (up to 2,000 contacts).

Can I migrate my Leadpages landing pages to another tool?

Most alternatives don't offer direct migration from Leadpages. You'll need to recreate pages using templates. However, Unbounce and Instapage offer concierge migration services on higher-tier plans to make the transition smoother.

Which Leadpages alternative has the best conversion optimization features?

Unbounce leads with its AI-powered Smart Traffic feature that automatically routes visitors to the highest-converting variant. Instapage is a close second with built-in heatmaps and advanced A/B testing.
