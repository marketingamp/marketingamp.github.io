5 Best Landing Page Builders to Increase Conversions (2026)

Updated May 12, 2026 • 13 min read

Your landing page is your digital salesperson. It works around the clock, never calls in sick, and either converts visitors into customers or lets them slip away. The difference between a 2% conversion rate and a 5% conversion rate can mean hundreds of thousands of dollars over a year.

We tested five of the most popular landing page builders on the same campaign: a webinar registration page with email capture, countdown timer, and testimonial section. Here's how each tool performed on design flexibility, A/B testing, integrations, and price.

1. Leadpages — Best for Small Businesses

Leadpages has carved out a strong position as the landing page builder that balances power with simplicity. You don't need to be a designer or developer to create professional-looking pages that actually convert.

The template library is the largest on this list with over 250 professionally designed options, all organized by industry and conversion goal. Each template has been tested for performance, so you're starting from a proven foundation rather than guessing.

What makes Leadpages particularly appealing for small businesses is the pricing. At $49/month for the Standard plan, it's the most affordable full-featured landing page builder. You also get unlimited page publishing, which means you can create as many landing pages as you need without worrying about page limits.

The built-in A/B testing is straightforward — duplicate a page, make changes, and let Leadpages split traffic automatically. It's not as sophisticated as Unbounce's AI-powered optimization, but it gets the job done for most businesses.

2. Unbounce — Best for Conversion Optimization

Unbounce is the tool you choose when conversion rate is your primary metric. Its standout feature is Smart Traffic, an AI system that automatically routes each visitor to the page variant most likely to convert them based on their attributes (device, location, browser, etc.).

In our testing, Smart Traffic consistently outperformed standard A/B testing by routing visitors more intelligently. Traditional A/B testing requires hundreds or thousands of visitors before reaching statistical significance. Smart Traffic starts optimizing from the first visitor.

The pixel-perfect drag-and-drop builder gives you complete design freedom — you can place elements anywhere on the page without being constrained by a grid. This is great for designers but can be overwhelming for beginners who prefer guardrails.

At $99/month for the Launch plan, Unbounce is pricier than Leadpages. But if you're spending money driving paid traffic, the conversion lift from Smart Traffic can easily pay for the difference.

3. Instapage — Best for Agencies and Enterprise

Instapage positions itself as the post-click optimization platform, and it delivers features that agencies and larger teams need: real-time collaboration (think Google Docs for landing pages), heatmaps, and a robust experimentation framework.

The collaboration features are what set Instapage apart. Multiple team members can work on the same page simultaneously, leave comments, and track revisions. For agencies managing landing pages across multiple clients, this workflow is invaluable.

Built-in heatmaps show you exactly where visitors are clicking, scrolling, and dropping off — without needing a separate tool like Hotjar. This data directly informs your optimization decisions.

4. ClickFunnels — Best for Full Sales Funnels

ClickFunnels is more than a landing page builder — it's a complete sales funnel platform. If you need landing pages connected to order forms, upsell pages, membership areas, and email sequences, ClickFunnels handles the entire flow.

The trade-off is complexity and cost. At $147/month, it's the most expensive option on this list. And for straightforward landing pages, it's overkill. But if you're building multi-step funnels — especially for digital products, courses, or high-ticket services — the integrated approach saves you from stitching together multiple tools.

5. Carrd — Best Budget Option

Carrd is the wildcard on this list. At just $19 per year (not per month), it's absurdly affordable. The catch: it only builds one-page sites. No multi-step funnels, no A/B testing, no AI optimization.

But for simple use cases — a lead capture page, a product launch page, a "coming soon" page — Carrd delivers. The designs are clean and modern, the builder is intuitive, and it loads incredibly fast. If you need a quick landing page and your budget is near zero, Carrd punches well above its weight.

Which Landing Page Builder Should You Choose?

Our Verdict

Best overall for small businesses: Leadpages. The combination of 250+ templates, affordable pricing, and unlimited pages makes it the smart default choice.

Best for paid traffic campaigns: Unbounce. Smart Traffic AI can significantly improve ROI on ad spend by automatically optimizing which page variant each visitor sees.

Best for agencies: Instapage. Real-time collaboration and built-in heatmaps streamline the multi-client workflow.

Best for full funnels: ClickFunnels. If you need more than landing pages, it's the integrated solution.

Best on a tight budget: Carrd. At $19/year, it's practically free — perfect for simple landing pages.
