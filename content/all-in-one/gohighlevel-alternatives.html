7 Best GoHighLevel Alternatives for Agencies & Marketers (2026)

Updated May 21, 2026 • 15 min read

GoHighLevel has become the go-to platform for marketing agencies. CRM, funnels, email, SMS, reputation management, white-label SaaS — it packs an enormous amount into one dashboard. We covered the full platform in our GoHighLevel review, and there's no question it delivers value for agencies willing to learn the system.

But it's not for everyone. The learning curve is steep, the interface can feel overwhelming, and at $97–$497/month, it's a significant investment — especially if you only need a few of its features. Maybe you're a solopreneur who doesn't need white-label client portals. Maybe you want deeper CRM capabilities than GoHighLevel offers. Or maybe you've hit limitations with its funnel builder and want something more polished.

Whatever the reason, we spent four weeks testing seven alternatives across the same criteria: CRM depth, funnel/page building, email and automation, agency features, and total cost. Here's the breakdown.

1. ClickFunnels — Best for Funnel-Focused Businesses

Our verdict: If funnels are the core of your business and you don't need deep CRM or agency white-labeling, ClickFunnels builds better-converting pages than GoHighLevel out of the box.

We compared the two platforms head-to-head in our GoHighLevel vs ClickFunnels breakdown, and the conclusion holds: ClickFunnels wins on funnel design and conversion optimization, while GoHighLevel wins on breadth. If you only care about the funnel side, ClickFunnels is the sharper tool.

The 2.0 rebuild brought a cleaner editor, better page load speeds, and built-in CRM and email features that close the gap with all-in-one platforms. You get a visual automation builder, A/B testing, membership areas, and checkout pages — all inside one dashboard. For the full breakdown, see our ClickFunnels review.

Key Features

  • Drag-and-drop funnel and page builder with 100+ templates
  • Built-in email marketing and visual automation workflows
  • One-click upsells and order bump functionality
  • Membership site hosting and course delivery
  • A/B split testing on every funnel step
  • Blog and basic CRM included in all plans

Pricing

ClickFunnels starts at $97/month (Startup) for 3 workspaces and 20 funnels. The Pro plan at $297/month removes limits and adds priority support. Annual billing saves roughly 16%.

Pros

  • Best-in-class funnel builder with proven conversion templates
  • Large community and extensive training library
  • Clean, modern editor after the 2.0 rebuild
  • Strong checkout and payment processing

Cons

  • No white-label or agency sub-accounts
  • CRM is basic compared to dedicated platforms
  • No SMS marketing or reputation management
  • $97/month entry price is steep for beginners

Best for: Entrepreneurs, course creators, and marketers who live and die by funnels and want the best conversion-focused builder available.

2. Systeme.io — Best Free Alternative

Our verdict: The most generous free plan in the space. If you're a solopreneur or just starting out and GoHighLevel's $97/month feels like too much, Systeme.io lets you build funnels, send emails, and sell courses without spending a cent.

We covered the platform in detail in our Systeme.io review, and the value proposition hasn't changed: it does 80% of what GoHighLevel does for agencies that don't need white-labeling or SMS. The free plan includes 3 funnels, 1 membership site, unlimited emails to up to 2,000 contacts, and a built-in affiliate program manager.

Key Features

  • Funnel builder with sales pages, opt-in pages, and checkout
  • Email marketing with automation rules and sequences
  • Online course hosting and membership sites
  • Built-in affiliate program management
  • Blog and custom domain support
  • One-click upsells, order bumps, and A/B testing

Pricing

Free plan: 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, unlimited emails. Startup: $27/month (5,000 contacts, 10 funnels). Webinar: $47/month. Unlimited: $97/month. All paid plans include more automations and community features.

Pros

  • Truly free plan with no credit card required
  • Covers funnels, email, courses, and affiliates in one tool
  • Simple interface with a short learning curve
  • Paid plans are significantly cheaper than GoHighLevel

Cons

  • No CRM pipeline management
  • No SMS or phone features
  • No white-label or agency sub-account options
  • Template designs are more basic than ClickFunnels or Kartra

Best for: Solopreneurs, course creators, and bootstrapped businesses who want an all-in-one platform without the price tag. A strong pick if you're coming from GoHighLevel and realizing you don't use half the features.

3. HubSpot — Best Enterprise CRM

Our verdict: If your main complaint about GoHighLevel is that the CRM feels shallow, HubSpot is the answer. Its CRM is leagues deeper — deal pipelines, custom objects, reporting dashboards, and a massive integration ecosystem. The trade-off is price: once you need marketing automation, costs escalate quickly.

We broke down the full platform in our HubSpot review. The free CRM is genuinely useful for contact management and basic deal tracking. But the Marketing Hub, Sales Hub, and Service Hub are where the real power lives — and where the bills start adding up.

Key Features

  • Full-featured CRM with custom objects and deal pipelines
  • Marketing automation with workflows and lead scoring
  • Landing page builder and blog hosting
  • Email marketing with smart personalization
  • Reporting dashboards and attribution modeling
  • 1,600+ integrations in the app marketplace

Pricing

Free CRM: unlimited contacts with basic features. Starter: $20/month per seat. Professional: $890/month (Marketing Hub). Enterprise: $3,600/month. HubSpot gets expensive fast at higher tiers, but the free and Starter plans are competitive for small teams.

Pros

  • Industry-leading CRM depth and reporting
  • Massive integration ecosystem
  • Free CRM tier is genuinely useful
  • Scales from startup to enterprise

Cons

  • Marketing automation requires expensive Professional plan
  • No white-label or agency reseller model
  • No built-in funnel builder (landing pages only)
  • Per-seat pricing adds up for larger teams

Best for: Growing businesses and sales teams that need a CRM-first platform with deep reporting, integrations, and scalability. Also a strong choice if you're looking for the best CRM for small business.

4. Kartra — Best for Course Creators + Marketing

Our verdict: Kartra sits in the sweet spot between GoHighLevel's agency focus and Systeme.io's simplicity. It's a polished all-in-one platform built for online businesses that sell courses, coaching, and digital products. The page builder is excellent, the automation engine is visual and powerful, and the helpdesk feature is a unique bonus.

Key Features

  • Drag-and-drop page and funnel builder with high-quality templates
  • Advanced behavioral automation with if/then logic
  • Membership portal and course hosting
  • Built-in helpdesk and ticket system
  • Video hosting with in-video CTAs
  • Affiliate management and tracking
  • Checkout pages with order bumps and payment plans

Pricing

Starter: $99/month (2,500 leads, 1 custom domain). Growth: $189/month (12,500 leads, 3 domains). Professional: $429/month (25,000 leads, 5 domains). All plans include every feature — limits are based on contacts and bandwidth.

Pros

  • All features included on every plan (no feature gating)
  • Beautiful page templates and polished editor
  • Built-in helpdesk is rare for all-in-one platforms
  • Strong video hosting with behavioral triggers

Cons

  • No SMS marketing or phone system
  • No white-label or agency sub-accounts
  • Contact limits on lower plans feel restrictive
  • Fewer integrations than HubSpot or ActiveCampaign

Best for: Course creators, coaches, and digital product sellers who want a single platform for pages, email, courses, and customer support without the agency overhead of GoHighLevel.

5. Vendasta — Best for White-Label Agencies

Our verdict: If your agency model depends on reselling software under your own brand, Vendasta is the most direct GoHighLevel competitor. It offers a white-label marketplace of 250+ products (reputation management, listings, social, advertising, websites) that you can bundle and sell to local business clients. Where GoHighLevel gives you one platform to white-label, Vendasta gives you an entire product catalog.

Key Features

  • White-label marketplace with 250+ resellable products
  • CRM and sales pipeline built for agency workflows
  • Automated client reporting and business intelligence
  • Reputation management and review generation
  • Listing distribution and local SEO tools
  • Social media management and content publishing
  • Task management and fulfillment workflows

Pricing

Free plan available with limited features. Essentials: $79/month. Professional: $399/month. Premium: $799/month. Custom enterprise pricing available. Revenue share model on marketplace products means you earn margin on everything you resell.

Pros

  • Massive white-label product catalog — far broader than GoHighLevel
  • Revenue share model lets agencies profit on resold tools
  • Strong local business and reputation management features
  • Client-facing dashboard and automated reporting

Cons

  • Complex platform with a significant learning curve
  • Funnel and page building is weaker than GoHighLevel
  • Higher-tier plans required for most useful features
  • Marketplace product costs add up beyond the base subscription

Best for: Agencies serving local businesses that want to white-label and resell a full suite of marketing tools. Particularly strong for reputation management and listings-focused agencies.

6. ActiveCampaign — Best for Email Automation

Our verdict: GoHighLevel has email and automation, but ActiveCampaign does both at a significantly higher level. The visual automation builder is the best in the industry, with conditional logic, split actions, goal tracking, and predictive sending that GoHighLevel can't match. If email is your primary marketing channel, this is the upgrade.

Key Features

  • Visual automation builder with 900+ automation recipes
  • Email marketing with dynamic content and predictive sending
  • Built-in CRM with deal pipelines and lead scoring
  • Site and event tracking for behavioral triggers
  • SMS marketing (on higher plans)
  • Landing page builder and form creation
  • Integration with 900+ third-party tools

Pricing

Starter: $15/month (1,000 contacts). Plus: $49/month (adds CRM, landing pages, SMS). Pro: $79/month (adds predictive sending and attribution). Enterprise: $145/month. All prices based on 1,000 contacts and scale with list size.

Pros

  • Best-in-class email automation and workflow builder
  • Affordable entry point at $15/month
  • CRM included on Plus plan and above
  • Excellent deliverability rates

Cons

  • No funnel builder — landing pages only
  • No white-label or agency sub-accounts
  • No membership sites or course hosting
  • Price increases significantly as contact list grows

Best for: Marketers and businesses where email is the primary revenue driver. Excellent for e-commerce, SaaS, and content businesses that need sophisticated automation without the full agency platform.

7. Keap (Infusionsoft) — Best for Small Business CRM

Our verdict: Keap is the veteran of CRM-driven marketing automation. It's been doing what GoHighLevel does — combining CRM, email, automation, and payments — since long before GoHighLevel existed. The platform is more polished and stable, with better invoicing and payment features. The downside: it's expensive and lacks the agency-specific tools that make GoHighLevel appealing to that market.

Key Features

  • CRM with contact management, tagging, and lead scoring
  • Visual sales pipeline and deal management
  • Email marketing with automation sequences
  • Invoicing, quotes, and payment processing
  • Appointment scheduling and booking
  • Text messaging and business phone line
  • E-commerce with checkout and order management

Pricing

Keap starts at $249/month for 1,500 contacts and 2 users. The Max plan at $349/month adds advanced automation, lead scoring, and promo codes. Additional users cost $29/month each. There's a 14-day free trial available.

Pros

  • Mature, stable platform with years of refinement
  • Strong invoicing and payment features
  • Built-in phone line and text messaging
  • Good for service businesses with appointment scheduling

Cons

  • Expensive — $249/month minimum is higher than GoHighLevel
  • No white-label or agency sub-accounts
  • No funnel builder or landing page builder
  • Contact limits feel restrictive for the price

Best for: Small businesses (especially service-based) that need a combined CRM, invoicing, and marketing automation tool. A solid pick if you're choosing between Keap and GoHighLevel and don't need agency features.

How We Tested These GoHighLevel Alternatives

We evaluated each platform across five categories:

  • CRM & Contact Management: Pipeline views, custom fields, tagging, lead scoring, and contact record depth.
  • Funnel & Page Building: Editor quality, template variety, mobile responsiveness, and conversion features like A/B testing.
  • Email & Automation: Sequence builders, visual workflows, deliverability, and the depth of conditional logic available.
  • Agency Features: White-labeling, sub-accounts, client management, and reseller capabilities.
  • Pricing & Value: Entry cost, scaling costs as contacts grow, and what you actually get for the money.

Every platform was tested with real accounts over a four-week period. We built funnels, sent email sequences, set up automations, and evaluated each tool against GoHighLevel's feature set to give you an honest comparison.

Which GoHighLevel Alternative Should You Choose?

The right choice depends on what you actually need:

  • You run an agency and need white-labeling: Vendasta offers a broader white-label marketplace. GoHighLevel is still strong here — see our full review before switching.
  • You want the best funnel builder: ClickFunnels builds higher-converting funnels out of the box.
  • You need a free starting point: Systeme.io gives you funnels, email, and courses at zero cost.
  • You need enterprise-grade CRM: HubSpot has the deepest CRM and reporting in this list.
  • You sell courses or digital products: Kartra balances page building, automation, and course delivery.
  • Email automation is your priority: ActiveCampaign has the most powerful automation engine at the lowest entry price.
  • You're a small service business: Keap combines CRM, invoicing, and scheduling in one mature platform.

No single platform replaces every GoHighLevel feature. But unless you're using all of GoHighLevel's capabilities — white-labeling, SMS, funnels, CRM, reputation management, and more — you're likely paying for features you don't touch. Switching to a specialized tool that matches your actual workflow can save money and reduce complexity at the same time.
