Hidden Costs to Watch For

Beyond the subscription, here are costs that can add up:

Payment processing: Stripe/PayPal fees (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction) are separate from your ClickFunnels subscription.

Custom domain: You'll need to buy your own domain ($10-15/year) — ClickFunnels doesn't include domain registration.

Funnel templates: While basic templates are free, premium marketplace templates can cost $97-$497 each.

Training courses: ClickFunnels pushes various training programs ($997-$2,497) — these aren't required but are heavily marketed.

Contact overages: If you exceed your plan's contact limit, you'll need to upgrade or pay overage fees.

Which Plan Should You Choose?

Choose Basic ($97/mo) if: You're a solo entrepreneur with 1-2 core products, don't need an affiliate program, and want to test the platform without a massive commitment.

Choose Pro ($297/mo) if: You run multiple businesses or products, want affiliates promoting your offers, need team access, or are hitting the Basic plan limits.

Consider alternatives if: You only need landing pages (try Leadpages at $49/mo), only need funnels on a budget (try Systeme.io for free), or find $97/mo too steep for your stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does ClickFunnels cost per month?

ClickFunnels starts at $97/month for the Basic plan and $297/month for the Pro plan. Annual billing reduces these to $81/month and $248/month respectively — a 16% discount.

Does ClickFunnels have a free plan?

No. ClickFunnels offers a 14-day free trial but no permanent free plan. If you need a free funnel builder, Systeme.io is the best alternative with a generous free tier.

Is ClickFunnels worth the price?

It depends on your usage. If you actively use funnels, email marketing, and course hosting, paying $97/mo for one tool is cheaper than $150+/mo for separate tools. But if you only need basic landing pages, you're overpaying significantly.
