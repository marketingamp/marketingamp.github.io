Shopify Pricing 2026: Every Plan Compared (+ Transaction Fees Explained)

Shopify's monthly fee is just the starting point. Transaction fees, apps, and themes can significantly change your total cost. Here's the full picture.

Last updated: May 2026 · By Dave Hassell

Shopify powers over 4 million online stores worldwide, but its pricing structure trips up a lot of new merchants. The subscription is straightforward — $39 to $399/month — but the real cost depends on your payment processor, which apps you need, and your sales volume.

Starter — $5/month

$5/month

The Starter plan doesn't include a full online store. Instead, you get product pages and checkout links you can share on social media, messaging apps, or embed on an existing website. Think of it as a payment link service, not an e-commerce platform.

  • Product pages with checkout
  • Shareable product links
  • Shopify Inbox (chat)
  • Order management
  • 5% + $0.30 per transaction

Best for: Creators selling 1-2 products on social media who don't need a full store. The 5% transaction fee is steep — at $1,000/mo revenue, you're paying $55 in fees plus $5 subscription.

Basic — $39/month

$39/month

Basic is the real starting point for most merchants. You get a full online store, unlimited products, and reasonable transaction rates. Annual billing drops this to $29/month — a meaningful savings for new stores.

  • Full online store with all themes
  • Unlimited products
  • 2 staff accounts
  • 10 inventory locations
  • 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (Shopify Payments)
  • Up to 77% shipping discount
  • Manual order creation
  • Discount codes
  • Free SSL certificate
  • Abandoned cart recovery

Best for: New and small e-commerce businesses doing under $10,000/month in sales. The 2.9% rate is industry-standard and the shipping discounts provide real savings.

Shopify — $105/month

$105/month

The mid-tier plan drops your payment processing rate to 2.6% and adds professional reports and 5 staff accounts. The lower transaction rate starts saving money around $10,000+/month in sales.

  • Everything in Basic, plus:
  • 5 staff accounts
  • 2.6% + $0.30 per transaction
  • Professional analytics reports
  • Up to 88% shipping discount
  • USPS Priority Mail Cubic pricing

Best for: Growing stores doing $10,000-$50,000/month. At $20,000/mo revenue, the 0.3% rate reduction saves $60/month — more than covering the $66/month price difference from Basic.

Advanced — $399/month

$399/month

Advanced is for high-volume merchants who benefit from the lowest transaction rates (2.4%) and need advanced reporting, 15 staff accounts, and estimated duties for international selling.

  • Everything in Shopify, plus:
  • 15 staff accounts
  • 2.4% + $0.30 per transaction
  • Advanced report builder
  • Estimated duties & import taxes
  • Third-party calculated shipping
  • 0.6% third-party gateway fee (lowest)

Best for: Established stores doing $50,000+/month. At $100k/month revenue, the 0.2% rate reduction from Shopify tier saves $200/month.

When to Upgrade: The Break-Even Math

The only reason to upgrade plans is lower transaction fees (assuming you use Shopify Payments). Here's the math:

UpgradeMonthly Fee IncreaseRate SavingsBreak-Even Revenue
Basic → Shopify+$66/mo0.3% per sale$22,000/mo
Shopify → Advanced+$294/mo0.2% per sale$147,000/mo

If your monthly revenue is below $22,000, stick with Basic. If it's below $147,000, stick with Shopify. The math is that simple.

Hidden Costs to Budget For

Apps: Most stores need 5-10 apps for reviews, SEO, email marketing, etc. Budget $50-200/month for essential apps.

Theme: Free themes work fine, but premium themes ($180-$350 one-time) offer more customization and features.

Domain: $14/year through Shopify or ~$10/year through a registrar like Namecheap.

Shopify Email: First 10,000 emails/month free, then $1 per 1,000 additional emails.

Transaction fees: Even at 2.9%, a $50,000/month store pays ~$1,480 in processing fees.

Which Plan Should You Choose?

Choose Starter ($5/mo) if: You're selling 1-2 products on social media and don't need a full store. Be aware the 5% fee adds up fast.

Choose Basic ($39/mo) if: You're launching a real online store. This plan covers everything 90% of new merchants need. Pay annually to drop it to $29/mo.

Choose Shopify ($105/mo) if: Your monthly revenue exceeds $22,000 and you need professional reports and more staff accounts.

Choose Advanced ($399/mo) if: You're doing $100,000+/month and sell internationally. The estimated duties feature prevents surprise costs for international buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Shopify cost per month?

Shopify costs $39/month (Basic), $105/month (Shopify), or $399/month (Advanced). There's also a $5/month Starter plan for social selling. Annual billing saves 25% on all plans.

What are Shopify's transaction fees?

With Shopify Payments: 2.9% + $0.30 (Basic), 2.6% + $0.30 (Shopify), or 2.4% + $0.30 (Advanced). Using a third-party gateway adds an extra 2%, 1%, or 0.6% on top.

Is Shopify worth it for a small business?

Yes. The Basic plan at $39/month includes everything needed to run a professional online store. The break-even point is roughly $500-1,000/month in sales to justify the subscription cost after factoring in transaction fees.
