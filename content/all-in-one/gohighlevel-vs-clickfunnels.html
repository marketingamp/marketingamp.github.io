GoHighLevel vs ClickFunnels 2026: Which Platform Is Actually Better?

GoHighLevel and ClickFunnels both promise to replace your marketing tech stack, but they serve different audiences. ClickFunnels is a funnel-first platform. GoHighLevel is a CRM-first platform that happens to include funnels. That distinction shapes everything about how they work.

I have tested both extensively — building funnels, running email campaigns, and managing client work. Here is where each platform wins and who should pick which.

Pricing Breakdown

Both platforms start at $97/month, but what you get at that price is very different.

GoHighLevel Starter ($97/mo) includes CRM, funnel builder, email marketing, SMS (usage-based), website builder, appointment calendar, pipeline management, reputation management, and one sub-account. The Unlimited plan ($297/mo) adds unlimited sub-accounts and an API. Agency Pro ($497/mo) adds white-label SaaS mode where you can resell the platform as your own.

ClickFunnels Basic ($97/mo) includes the funnel builder, basic email, course builder, and one website. The Pro plan ($297/mo) adds the affiliate center, API access, and removes funnel limits. No CRM, no SMS, no appointment booking, no reputation management — you would need separate tools for those.

When you account for the tools GoHighLevel replaces (CRM, email platform, SMS tool, booking software, review management), it replaces $300-500+/month in separate subscriptions.

Where GoHighLevel Wins

Agency features: GoHighLevel was purpose-built for marketing agencies. The sub-account system lets you manage dozens of clients from one dashboard. White-label SaaS mode (Agency Pro plan) lets you rebrand the entire platform and sell it to your clients as your own software — a genuine recurring revenue stream beyond services.

CRM and pipeline: GoHighLevel's CRM is a real sales tool — pipelines, deal stages, lead scoring, automated follow-ups. ClickFunnels has basic contact management, but it is not designed to manage a sales process end-to-end.

Communication channels: Built-in SMS, phone, voicemail drops, Facebook Messenger, and Google My Business messaging. ClickFunnels handles none of this natively.

Where ClickFunnels Wins

Funnel builder quality: ClickFunnels' page editor is more refined, with a larger template library, more granular A/B testing, and smoother drag-and-drop interactions. If your business is 100% funnel-driven and you need the best possible funnel creation experience, ClickFunnels delivers.

Community and training: The ClickFunnels ecosystem (FunnelFlix, One Funnel Away Challenge, active Facebook groups) is larger and more established. If you value learning resources alongside the tool, this matters.

Simplicity: ClickFunnels does fewer things, which means less complexity. If you don't need CRM, SMS, or agency features, ClickFunnels is easier to learn and faster to get results with.

The Verdict

Choose GoHighLevel if: You run an agency, manage multiple clients, need a CRM, want SMS and phone marketing, or want to white-label a SaaS platform. GoHighLevel replaces more tools and provides better value for service-based businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GoHighLevel better than ClickFunnels for agencies?

Yes. GoHighLevel was built specifically for agencies with features like white-label SaaS mode, unlimited sub-accounts, and the ability to resell the platform to clients. ClickFunnels is focused on individual funnel building and lacks agency-specific tools like white-labeling and client management.

Can I build funnels in GoHighLevel like ClickFunnels?

Yes. GoHighLevel includes a drag-and-drop funnel builder with templates, order forms, upsells, and A/B testing. The funnel builder is not as polished as ClickFunnels' editor, but it covers all the essential functionality and is included in the base price alongside CRM, email, SMS, and more.

Which is cheaper — GoHighLevel or ClickFunnels?

GoHighLevel starts at $97/month (same as ClickFunnels Basic), but includes CRM, email marketing, SMS, appointment booking, reputation management, and unlimited funnels. To get equivalent functionality with ClickFunnels, you would need the $297/month Pro plan plus additional third-party tools, making GoHighLevel significantly cheaper overall.
