ClickFunnels Review 2026: Is It Still Worth the Price?

Updated May 15, 2026 • 13 min read

ClickFunnels essentially invented the "sales funnel builder" category when it launched in 2014. Since then, a wave of competitors have emerged — many offering similar features at a fraction of the price. With Systeme.io offering funnels for free and GoHighLevel targeting the agency market, is ClickFunnels still worth $97+/month in 2026?

We built and ran real sales funnels on ClickFunnels for three months, processing actual transactions and comparing results against the alternatives. Here's our honest assessment.

What Is ClickFunnels?

ClickFunnels is a sales funnel builder that lets you create multi-step conversion sequences: opt-in pages, sales pages, order forms, upsell pages, thank-you pages, and membership areas. Since its 2.0 relaunch, it has expanded into a full business platform with email marketing, CRM, blog, website builder, course hosting, and affiliate management.

The core philosophy hasn't changed: instead of building a traditional website and hoping visitors find their way to a purchase, you build a deliberate funnel that guides each visitor through a specific path optimized for conversion.

The Funnel Builder: Still Best-in-Class

Despite all the competition, ClickFunnels still has the best funnel-building experience in the industry. The drag-and-drop editor is smooth and intuitive, the template library is the largest available (hundreds of templates across every funnel type), and the funnel flow visualization makes it easy to see and manage your entire conversion path.

Where ClickFunnels excels is in the details: one-click upsells and downsells work seamlessly with Stripe, order bump checkboxes are easy to add, A/B split testing is built into every page, and the conversion analytics show you exactly where visitors drop off in your funnel.

The templates are also notably better than competitors. They're designed by direct-response marketers who understand conversion optimization, not just graphic designers who make things look pretty. The difference shows up in actual conversion rates — in our testing, ClickFunnels templates converted 15-25% higher out of the box compared to generic landing page builder templates.

Email Marketing (Follow-Up Funnels)

ClickFunnels 2.0 includes built-in email marketing, which was previously a separate product. You can create email sequences, broadcast campaigns, and automation workflows triggered by funnel actions (opt-in, purchase, page visit, etc.).

The email builder is functional but not as refined as dedicated email marketing platforms. It handles the basics well: drip sequences, broadcasts, segmentation by tags, and basic automation. But it lacks advanced features like send-time optimization, detailed deliverability reporting, or the visual automation builders found in ActiveCampaign or GetResponse.

For most funnel-based businesses, the built-in email is adequate. Your welcome sequence, sales emails, and follow-up campaigns work fine. But if email marketing is a major revenue driver for your business, you might still want to pair ClickFunnels with a dedicated email tool.

CRM and Contact Management

The CRM in ClickFunnels 2.0 is a pipeline-style contact management system. You can track leads through custom stages, add notes, assign tasks, and see a complete activity timeline. It's functional for small businesses managing a sales pipeline, but it doesn't compete with dedicated CRMs like HubSpot or Salesforce for complex sales processes.

Membership Sites and Courses

ClickFunnels lets you create membership areas and host online courses. Members get login access to protected content organized into modules and lessons. You can drip-release content, create multiple membership tiers, and process payments through the same Stripe integration your funnels use.

The course builder is clean and functional, though it doesn't match dedicated course platforms like Kajabi or Teachable in terms of student experience or community features. For a bonus course bundled with a product, or a simple membership program, it works well. For a course-first business, a dedicated platform is likely a better fit.

Blog and Website Builder

ClickFunnels 2.0 added a full website builder and blog, moving beyond funnels-only. You can create a multi-page website with standard navigation, a blog for content marketing, and integrate everything with your funnels. The website builder uses the same drag-and-drop editor as the funnel builder, which makes it easy to learn.

It's a competent website builder, though it doesn't match the design flexibility or SEO tools of dedicated website builders. The real value is consolidation: your website and your funnels live on the same platform, sharing the same contacts, email system, and analytics.

The ClickFunnels Community

One underrated aspect of ClickFunnels is its community. Russell Brunson (the founder) has built an enormous ecosystem of training, books, events, and a passionate user community. The Facebook group has hundreds of thousands of members sharing funnels, strategies, and results.

This matters because funnel strategy is often more important than the tool itself. Having access to proven funnel templates, case studies, and a community of marketers who actively share what's working is a genuine advantage over platforms where you're mostly on your own.

Who Is ClickFunnels Best For?

Our Verdict

Ideal for: Entrepreneurs, info-product sellers, coaches, consultants, and direct-response marketers who rely on sales funnels as their primary revenue driver. If you're selling digital products, running webinar funnels, or using a value-ladder business model, ClickFunnels gives you the best funnel-building experience available. The community and training ecosystem are also valuable if you're still learning funnel strategy.

Not ideal for: Businesses on a tight budget (Systeme.io does 80% of what ClickFunnels does for free), agencies managing multiple clients (GoHighLevel is better architected for that), or businesses that primarily need email marketing with occasional landing pages (GetResponse or Kit are better fits).

Our take: ClickFunnels is no longer the only option, but it's still the best option for businesses that are serious about funnel-based selling. The editor, templates, and community remain unmatched. Whether the premium price is justified depends on how central funnels are to your business. If they're your bread and butter, ClickFunnels pays for itself quickly. If you're just starting out, try Systeme.io's free plan first and upgrade to ClickFunnels when your revenue justifies the investment.
