Free Plan — $0/month (Forever)

$0/month forever

Systeme.io's free plan is remarkably generous. You get 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, unlimited email sends, 1 online course, 1 community, and a custom domain — with no credit card required and no time limit. This is enough to launch and validate a digital product business.

  • 2,000 contacts with unlimited emails
  • 3 sales funnels (10 steps total)
  • 1 online course with unlimited students
  • 1 community
  • 1 blog
  • 1 custom domain
  • Affiliate program management
  • Order bumps and upsells
  • Stripe and PayPal integration
  • No transaction fees

Best for: Anyone starting out who wants to validate their offer before spending money on tools. You can genuinely build a profitable business on this plan.

Startup Plan — $27/month

$27/month

The Startup plan 2.5x your limits across the board. At $27/mo (or $19/mo annually), it's the logical next step once you hit 2,000 contacts or need more than 3 funnels.

  • 5,000 contacts
  • 10 sales funnels (50 steps)
  • 5 online courses
  • 5 communities
  • 10 automation rules
  • 3 custom domains
  • A/B testing
  • Coupon codes

Best for: Creators who've validated their offer and are growing past 2,000 contacts. The $27/mo is easily covered by a handful of product sales.

Webinar Plan — $47/month

$47/month

The Webinar plan adds evergreen webinar funnels and significantly more capacity. If webinars are part of your sales strategy (high-ticket coaching, courses, consulting), this tier unlocks automated webinar replays that sell while you sleep.

  • 10,000 contacts
  • 50 sales funnels (300 steps)
  • 20 online courses
  • Evergreen webinars
  • 100 automation rules
  • 10 custom domains
  • 20 communities

Best for: Course creators and coaches using webinars as their primary sales mechanism.

Unlimited Plan — $97/month

$97/month

The Unlimited plan removes all caps and lets you remove Systeme.io branding. At $97/mo it matches ClickFunnels' entry price while including email, courses, and communities that ClickFunnels charges extra for.

  • Unlimited contacts
  • Unlimited funnels and steps
  • Unlimited courses and communities
  • Unlimited automation rules
  • Unlimited custom domains
  • Remove Systeme.io branding
  • Priority support
  • 1-on-1 migration assistance

Best for: Established businesses with large audiences who want no limits and clean branding.

Which Plan Should You Choose?

Start with Free if: You're launching your first digital product, have under 2,000 contacts, and want to validate before investing. The free plan is genuinely functional — not a stripped-down demo.

Upgrade to Startup ($27/mo) when: You pass 2,000 contacts or need more than 3 funnels. At $19/mo on annual billing, this is a no-brainer for any profitable business.

Upgrade to Webinar ($47/mo) when: Evergreen webinars become part of your sales strategy. The automated webinar replay feature alone can generate significant passive revenue.

Upgrade to Unlimited ($97/mo) when: You want to remove branding limits and never worry about hitting caps as your business scales.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Systeme.io really free?

Yes. The free plan includes 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, unlimited emails, 1 course, and 1 custom domain with no credit card required and no time limit. It's not a trial — you can use it forever.

How much does Systeme.io cost?

Paid plans are $27/month (Startup), $47/month (Webinar), and $97/month (Unlimited). Annual billing saves 30% — the Startup plan drops to just $19/month equivalent.

Is Systeme.io better than ClickFunnels?

For most solopreneurs and small businesses, yes. Systeme.io offers 80% of ClickFunnels' functionality for free. ClickFunnels has more advanced funnel features and a larger marketplace, but at $97/mo vs. $0, Systeme.io wins on value for most users.
