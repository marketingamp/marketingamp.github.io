Starter Plan — $97/month

$97/month

The Starter plan gives you the full GoHighLevel platform for up to 3 client accounts (sub-accounts). If you're a solo agency owner managing a handful of clients, or a local business using it for your own marketing, this is where to start.

  • 3 sub-accounts (client accounts)
  • Unlimited contacts and users
  • CRM with pipeline management
  • Funnel and website builder
  • Email marketing (unlimited sends)
  • SMS and phone (usage-based pricing)
  • Appointment scheduling
  • Reputation management
  • Workflow automations
  • Invoicing and payments

Best for: New agency owners with 1-3 clients, or local businesses managing their own marketing.

Unlimited Plan — $297/month

$297/month

The Unlimited plan removes the sub-account cap and adds white-label branding for your desktop app. This is the most popular plan for growing agencies because you can add unlimited clients without per-seat costs eating into margins.

  • Everything in Starter, plus:
  • Unlimited sub-accounts
  • White-label desktop app
  • Custom domains for your branded platform
  • Branded login pages
  • Remove GHL branding entirely
  • Membership/course hosting

Best for: Agencies with 4+ clients who want to present the platform under their own brand. The economics make sense at 4+ clients since $297 ÷ 4 = $74/client vs. paying $97 per CRM seat elsewhere.

Agency Pro (SaaS Mode) — $497/month

$497/month

Agency Pro unlocks SaaS mode — you can resell GoHighLevel as your own software product with custom pricing, Stripe billing, and a white-labeled mobile app. This turns your agency into a SaaS company.

  • Everything in Unlimited, plus:
  • Full SaaS mode (resell as your own product)
  • Custom Stripe billing for clients
  • White-label mobile app (iOS + Android)
  • Automatic client onboarding
  • Usage-based rebilling to clients
  • Priority support

Best for: Agencies that want to build a SaaS revenue stream on top of their services — charging clients $297-$997/mo for "their" platform.

Usage-Based Costs (All Plans)

SMS, phone calls, and AI features are billed based on usage across all plans:

SMS: ~$0.0079 per segment (US). International rates vary.

Phone calls: ~$0.026/minute outbound, $0.0085/minute inbound (US).

Email: First 10,000/month free, then ~$0.675 per additional 1,000.

AI (Conversation AI): Varies by model — roughly $0.01-0.03 per interaction.

Most agencies spend $20-100/month on usage fees depending on volume.

Which Plan Should You Choose?

Choose Starter ($97/mo) if: You're managing 1-3 clients or using it for your own business. Get comfortable with the platform before scaling.

Choose Unlimited ($297/mo) if: You have 4+ agency clients and want to white-label the platform. At 5 clients paying $200/mo for your services, you're profiting $703/mo on the platform alone.

Choose Agency Pro ($497/mo) if: You want to sell GoHighLevel as your own SaaS product with automated billing and a branded mobile app.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does GoHighLevel cost?

GoHighLevel costs $97/month (Starter), $297/month (Unlimited), or $497/month (Agency Pro). Annual billing saves roughly 17% across all plans. Usage-based costs for SMS and phone are additional.

Does GoHighLevel have a free plan?

No free plan, but they offer a full-access 14-day free trial on all tiers. No credit card required for the trial in most cases.

Can I white-label GoHighLevel for my clients?

Yes. Desktop white-labeling is available on the Unlimited plan ($297/mo). Full white-labeling including a custom mobile app requires the Agency Pro plan ($497/mo).
