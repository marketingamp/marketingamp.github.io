7 Best ClickFunnels Alternatives That Cost Less (2026)

Updated May 12, 2026 • 14 min read

ClickFunnels is a powerful funnel builder, but at $147/month for the basic plan (and $297/month for the Pro tier), it's one of the most expensive tools in the marketing stack. For solopreneurs and small businesses, that's $1,764 per year before you've spent a dime on ads.

The good news: the funnel builder market has matured. Several competitors now offer comparable features — drag-and-drop builders, email automation, membership sites, checkout pages — at a fraction of the price. Some even have free plans.

We tested seven ClickFunnels alternatives on the same criteria: funnel building, landing page quality, email tools, checkout/payment processing, and total cost of ownership. Here's what we found.

Systeme.io is the closest thing to a free ClickFunnels replacement. Its free plan includes up to 3 sales funnels, 1 blog, 1 membership site, unlimited emails to up to 2,000 contacts, and a built-in checkout system. That's more than many paid tools offer at their entry tier.

The funnel builder is straightforward. You won't find the same depth of templates as ClickFunnels, but the drag-and-drop editor handles opt-in pages, sales pages, upsells, downsells, and thank-you pages without drama. The built-in email automation covers sequences, broadcasts, and tagging — enough to run a real business without bolting on a separate email platform.

For affiliates, Systeme.io stands out with a 60% lifetime recurring commission and a 180-day cookie window. That combination is among the most generous in the SaaS space.

2. Leadpages — Best for Landing Pages

If your primary need is high-converting landing pages rather than full multi-step funnels, Leadpages is hard to beat. It has one of the largest template libraries in the space — over 200 mobile-responsive templates — and its drag-and-drop builder is smoother than most competitors.

Leadpages shines in conversion optimization. Every template is sorted by conversion rate, so you're not guessing which designs perform. Built-in A/B testing, alert bars, and pop-ups come standard on all plans. At $37/month, you're saving over $100/month compared to ClickFunnels while getting a more focused landing page tool.

The trade-off: Leadpages doesn't include email marketing or membership sites. You'll need a separate email tool. But if you already use something like ConvertKit or Mailchimp, Leadpages slots right in via native integrations.

Pros

  • 200+ high-converting templates sorted by conversion rate

4. GrooveFunnels — Best Lifetime Deal

GrooveFunnels made its name with an aggressive lifetime deal — pay once and get access to the entire Groove platform forever. No monthly fees. For anyone who has been burned by rising subscription costs, that pitch is compelling.

The free plan includes a funnel builder (GroovePages), email marketing (GrooveMail), and a marketplace for selling digital products. It's limited in contacts and funnels, but it's functional enough to launch a basic digital product business at zero cost.

Where Groove excels is in the sheer breadth of tools included — funnels, email, video, helpdesk, memberships, webinars, and more.

5. Unbounce — Best for A/B Testing

Unbounce is the tool to pick if conversion rate optimization is your top priority. Its Smart Traffic feature uses AI to automatically route each visitor to the landing page variant most likely to convert them — based on their attributes like device, location, and referral source. Unbounce claims this can boost conversions by up to 30%.

The A/B testing capabilities go deeper than any other tool on this list. You can test headlines, layouts, CTAs, and entire page designs with statistical confidence tracking built in. For marketers spending serious money on paid traffic, even a small conversion lift pays for the tool many times over.

At $74/month, Unbounce saves you $73/month versus ClickFunnels. Its affiliate program offers 35% recurring commissions paid via PayPal.

Pros

  • AI-powered Smart Traffic for automatic optimization
  • Best-in-class A/B testing with statistical tracking
  • Dynamic text replacement for PPC campaigns
  • 35% recurring affiliate commission, paid via PayPal

Cons

  • No email marketing or membership features
  • Focused on landing pages, not full funnels

Which ClickFunnels Alternative Should You Choose?

Our Verdict

For maximum savings (free): Systeme.io is the clear winner. You get funnels, email, courses, and checkout at $0/month. It's the single best way to escape ClickFunnels pricing without sacrificing core functionality.

For landing page conversion: Leadpages delivers the best templates and built-in A/B testing at $37/month. Pair it with a separate email tool and you're still saving $80+/month vs ClickFunnels.

For replacing your entire stack: Kartra includes everything — funnels, email, helpdesk, video, memberships — in one platform. The $99/month price tag looks expensive until you add up all the tools it replaces.

For course creators: Kajabi is purpose-built for selling knowledge products. If courses or coaching are your business, stop paying for ClickFunnels and a separate course platform.

For the tightest budget: Builderall at $16.90/month gives you more tools per dollar than anything else on the market. It won't win design awards, but it works.
