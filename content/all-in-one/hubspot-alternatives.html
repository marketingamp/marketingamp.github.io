7 Best HubSpot Alternatives for Small Business (2026)

Updated May 16, 2026 • 16 min read

HubSpot's free CRM is genuinely excellent. The problem is everything around it. The moment you need marketing automation, custom reporting, or A/B testing, you're looking at the Marketing Hub Professional at $800/month. Add Sales Hub Professional ($90/user/month) and Service Hub ($90/user/month), and a small team of five can easily spend $2,000+/month.

For enterprise companies, HubSpot's ecosystem justifies the price. For small businesses doing $10K–$50K/month in revenue, it's overkill. You need a CRM that manages contacts, tracks deals, sends emails, and automates follow-ups — without costing more than your office rent.

We tested seven HubSpot alternatives on the features that matter most to small businesses: contact management, deal tracking, email marketing, automation, and reporting. Here's how they compare.

1. Zoho CRM — Best Overall Value

Zoho CRM is the most complete HubSpot alternative at the lowest price point. The free plan supports up to 3 users with contact management, deal tracking, tasks, and basic workflow rules. Paid plans start at $14/user/month and include email marketing, advanced automation, custom reports, and third-party integrations.

What makes Zoho particularly compelling is the broader Zoho ecosystem. Zoho One ($45/user/month) gives you access to 45+ business apps — CRM, email, project management, invoicing, helpdesk, HR, and more. It's the closest thing to HubSpot's all-in-one vision at a fraction of the cost.

The trade-off is polish. Zoho's interface isn't as clean as HubSpot's, and the learning curve is steeper due to the sheer number of configuration options. But for businesses willing to invest time in setup, the feature-to-price ratio is unmatched.

Pros

  • Free plan for 3 users with core CRM features
  • $14/user/mo is 85% cheaper than HubSpot's paid tiers
  • Zoho One bundles 45+ business apps for $45/user/mo
  • Highly customizable workflows and modules

Cons

  • Interface less polished than HubSpot
  • Steep learning curve with so many options
  • Some integrations require Zoho-specific connectors

2. Pipedrive — Best Visual Sales Pipeline

Pipedrive's claim to fame is the best visual pipeline interface in the CRM market. Every deal is a card you drag across stages, with color-coded indicators showing which deals need attention. It was designed by salespeople, not engineers, and it shows — the interface gets out of your way and lets you focus on closing.

At $14/user/month, Pipedrive includes contact management, deal tracking, email integration, activity scheduling, and custom reporting. The LeadBooster add-on ($32.50/month) adds chatbots, web forms, and a prospecting database for lead generation.

Where Pipedrive falls short compared to HubSpot is marketing. There's no built-in email marketing, landing pages, or content management. Pipedrive is a pure sales tool — it does that one thing exceptionally well but requires separate tools for marketing functions.

Pros

  • Best visual pipeline in the industry
  • Clean, intuitive interface designed for salespeople
  • Strong mobile app for on-the-go deal management
  • 400+ integrations via marketplace

Cons

  • No built-in email marketing or landing pages
  • Add-ons for lead gen and web visitors cost extra
  • Reporting less powerful than HubSpot on higher tiers

3. Freshsales — Best AI-Powered CRM

Freshsales (part of Freshworks) stands out with built-in AI features at prices that don't require an enterprise budget. Freddy AI scores leads based on engagement signals, suggests the best time to contact prospects, and can even predict deal outcomes. At $9/user/month for the Growth plan, you get AI capabilities that HubSpot reserves for its $800+/month tiers.

The free plan supports up to 3 users with contact management, built-in phone, email, and chat. The $9/month Growth plan adds visual pipelines, AI scoring, custom fields, and workflow automation. At $39/user/month, the Pro plan adds multiple pipelines, AI-powered deal insights, and advanced analytics.

Freshsales integrates natively with other Freshworks products — Freshdesk (support), Freshmarketer (marketing), and Freshservice (IT) — creating a mini-HubSpot ecosystem at SMB prices.

Pros

  • AI lead scoring and deal predictions from $9/user/mo
  • Free plan with phone, email, and chat built in
  • Freshworks ecosystem for support and marketing
  • Clean, modern interface

Cons

  • Fewer integrations than HubSpot or Salesforce
  • Marketing features require separate Freshmarketer product
  • Advanced reporting only on Pro plan ($39/user/mo)

4. Monday CRM — Most Customizable Views

Monday.com started as a project management tool and expanded into CRM. Its biggest strength is flexibility — you can view your pipeline as a Kanban board, table, timeline, calendar, chart, or map. No other CRM on this list offers this many visualization options for the same data.

The CRM module includes lead management, deal tracking, email sync, activity logging, and customizable dashboards. Automation recipes let you build workflows without code — "When deal stage changes to Won, notify the team and create an invoice in QuickBooks." The recipes are simpler to set up than HubSpot's workflows.

At $12/seat/month (minimum 3 seats = $36/month), Monday CRM is affordable for small teams. The main limitation is that it's a CRM built on top of a project management platform, so some sales-specific features (like lead scoring and predictive analytics) aren't as deep as dedicated CRM tools.

Pros

  • Most flexible view options (Kanban, table, timeline, chart, map)
  • No-code automation recipes are easy to build
  • Seamless integration with Monday.com project management
  • Beautiful, colorful interface

Cons

  • Minimum 3-seat purchase ($36/mo minimum)
  • Less depth in sales-specific features
  • No built-in email marketing

5. Salesforce Essentials — Enterprise CRM at SMB Price

Salesforce Essentials brings the world's most popular CRM to small businesses at $25/user/month. You get the core Salesforce platform — contact management, opportunity tracking, customizable dashboards, and the AppExchange marketplace with thousands of integrations. It's the same foundation that Fortune 500 companies run on, scaled down for teams under 10.

The key advantage is growth headroom. If your business scales, you can upgrade to Salesforce's full platform without migrating to a different CRM. Every other tool on this list would require a migration if you outgrow it. With Salesforce, you just upgrade your plan.

6. HoneyBook — Best for Service Businesses

HoneyBook isn't a traditional CRM — it's a client management platform built for freelancers, consultants, photographers, designers, and other service professionals. It combines proposals, contracts, invoicing, scheduling, and payments into one workflow. Send a proposal, get it signed, collect a deposit, and schedule the kickoff call — all from one platform.

At $19/month (flat, not per-user), HoneyBook is one of the most affordable options for solo service providers. The $39/month Premium plan adds automation, custom branding, and priority support. Unlike HubSpot, which charges per user and per feature hub, HoneyBook's pricing is simple and predictable.

7. GoHighLevel — Best for Agencies

GoHighLevel is the HubSpot alternative built specifically for marketing agencies. At $97/month (unlimited contacts, unlimited users), it includes CRM, email marketing, SMS, funnel builder, website builder, appointment scheduling, reputation management, and — crucially — full white-labeling. You can resell GoHighLevel to your clients under your own brand.

The $297/month Agency Unlimited plan lets you create unlimited sub-accounts for clients, each with their own login, funnels, and automations. This is the feature that makes GoHighLevel the platform of choice for agencies: instead of cobbling together HubSpot + ClickFunnels + Calendly + Podium, you get everything in one tool that you can rebrand and resell.

For non-agency businesses, GoHighLevel's breadth of features at $97/month still undercuts HubSpot's paid tiers significantly. The affiliate program offers 40% recurring commissions on the lifetime of every customer, making it one of the most lucrative in the SaaS space.

Which HubSpot Alternative Should You Choose?

Our Verdict

Best overall value: Zoho CRM. Free for 3 users, $14/user/mo paid, with the broadest feature set per dollar. The Zoho One ecosystem at $45/user/mo is the closest thing to HubSpot's all-in-one vision at an SMB price.

Best for sales teams: Pipedrive. The visual pipeline is the best in the business. If your primary need is deal tracking and sales workflow, nothing else is as intuitive.

Best for agencies: GoHighLevel. White-label everything, unlimited sub-accounts, and a feature set that replaces 5+ tools. No other CRM offers this for agencies at $97/mo.

Best for service businesses: HoneyBook. Proposals, contracts, invoicing, and scheduling in one flow at a flat $19/mo. Built for how freelancers and consultants actually work.

Best for budget-conscious teams: Freshsales at $9/user/mo with AI lead scoring. The cheapest paid CRM with meaningful AI features.
