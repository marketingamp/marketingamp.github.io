7 Best Systeme.io Alternatives for Marketing & Funnels (2026)

Updated May 21, 2026 • 14 min read

Systeme.io has earned its reputation as the scrappy all-in-one marketing platform that gives you funnels, email, courses, and automation on a genuinely free plan. As we covered in our full Systeme.io review, it's a remarkable value proposition for bootstrapped founders.

But it's not the right fit for everyone. Maybe you've outgrown the contact limits. Maybe you need more sophisticated A/B testing, a built-in CRM, or agency-level white-labeling. Perhaps your course business demands a premium student experience that Systeme.io's interface can't deliver.

Whatever the reason, the market has options. We spent three weeks testing seven Systeme.io alternatives across the features that matter most: funnel building, email automation, course hosting, checkout/payments, and overall value. We built real funnels, sent real emails, and compared the results side by side.

Here's what we found — and which platform makes sense depending on your goals and budget. If you're specifically comparing funnel builders, our best sales funnel builders roundup goes deeper on that angle.

1. ClickFunnels — Best for Dedicated Funnel Building

Verdict: If funnels are your core business activity and you want the most refined funnel-building experience available, ClickFunnels remains the gold standard. It costs significantly more than Systeme.io, but the funnel workflow, template library, and split-testing capabilities justify it for serious marketers.

ClickFunnels essentially invented the modern sales funnel builder category, and its 2.0 platform has matured considerably. The drag-and-drop editor feels polished, the funnel templates cover everything from simple opt-in pages to complex webinar sequences, and the built-in A/B testing lets you optimize every step of your funnel without third-party tools.

Where ClickFunnels pulls ahead of Systeme.io is in its funnel-specific thinking. Every feature — from the editor to the analytics dashboard — is designed around the concept of a multi-step funnel. You get visual funnel maps, per-step conversion tracking, and one-click upsell/downsell pages that integrate natively with Stripe and PayPal. For a deeper dive, see our ClickFunnels review or our Systeme.io vs ClickFunnels comparison.

Pricing: Starts at $81/month (Startup plan) with up to 3 workspaces and 1 domain. The Pro plan at $248/month unlocks unlimited funnels and advanced analytics. Check our ClickFunnels pricing breakdown for full details.

Pros: Industry-leading funnel builder, excellent split testing, strong template library, active community and training resources, native checkout with order bumps.

Cons: Expensive compared to Systeme.io, email marketing features are basic compared to dedicated platforms, learning curve for beginners.

Best for: Marketers and entrepreneurs whose revenue depends directly on funnel performance and who need granular conversion optimization tools.

2. GoHighLevel — Best for Agencies

Verdict: GoHighLevel is the platform to choose if you run a marketing agency or want to resell marketing tools under your own brand. Its white-label SaaS mode turns GoHighLevel into your own branded software product — something no other platform on this list offers.

GoHighLevel combines a CRM, funnel builder, email and SMS marketing, appointment scheduling, reputation management, and pipeline tracking into one dashboard. It's not trying to be the prettiest tool in any single category. Instead, it aims to replace the entire stack an agency would otherwise cobble together from five or six separate subscriptions.

Compared to Systeme.io, GoHighLevel adds a full CRM with pipeline management, two-way SMS, call tracking, and review automation. The funnel builder is capable though not quite as intuitive as ClickFunnels. Where GoHighLevel truly shines is the agency model: you can create unlimited sub-accounts for clients, each with their own login, and charge them whatever you want. Read our GoHighLevel review for the full breakdown.

Pricing: Starts at $97/month (Starter) with limited features. The $297/month Unlimited plan is where most agencies land, giving you unlimited sub-accounts and the SaaS mode. See our GoHighLevel pricing guide for a detailed comparison of plans.

Pros: White-label capability, built-in CRM with pipelines, SMS and call tracking, unlimited sub-accounts on higher plans, all-in-one agency toolkit.

Cons: Steep learning curve, interface can feel cluttered, overkill for solo creators, email deliverability requires careful setup.

Best for: Marketing agencies managing multiple clients, consultants who want to resell marketing tools, and businesses that need CRM plus marketing automation in one place.

3. Kartra — Best for Course Creators

Verdict: Kartra strikes the best balance between funnel building and course delivery. Its behavioral automation engine is genuinely impressive — you can trigger sequences based on video watch time, page visits, or membership progress, giving you granular control that Systeme.io can't match.

Kartra bundles pages, funnels, email, memberships, helpdesk, video hosting, and calendars into a single platform. What sets it apart from the crowd is the depth of its automation. You can build "if/then" behavioral sequences that respond to exactly how a prospect or student interacts with your content. Watched 75% of a sales video? Trigger a specific follow-up email. Completed Module 3 of your course? Unlock a bonus offer.

The membership and course portal in Kartra is more polished than what Systeme.io offers. You get drip content, progress tracking, community features, and the ability to bundle courses into different membership tiers. The checkout system supports subscriptions, payment plans, trials, and one-click upsells.

Pricing: Starts at $99/month (Starter) for up to 2,500 leads. The Growth plan at $189/month expands to 12,500 leads and removes most limitations. All plans include the full feature set — no paywalled features.

Pros: Sophisticated behavioral automation, solid course and membership tools, built-in video hosting, helpdesk included, all features available on every plan.

Cons: No free plan, page builder feels slightly dated compared to newer tools, limited third-party integrations, smaller template library.

Best for: Course creators, coaches, and membership site owners who want deep automation tied to student behavior and content consumption.

4. Leadpages — Best for Landing Pages

Verdict: If your primary need is high-converting landing pages rather than a full all-in-one suite, Leadpages delivers the best page-building experience on this list. Its templates consistently outperform what we've built in Systeme.io in terms of design quality and mobile responsiveness.

Leadpages is more focused than the other tools here. It does landing pages, pop-ups, alert bars, and basic checkout — and it does them exceptionally well. The template library is extensive and organized by industry and conversion goal. The drag-and-drop builder is fast, intuitive, and produces clean-looking pages without any CSS tweaking.

Where Leadpages won't replace Systeme.io is in course hosting, membership sites, or complex funnel sequences. It's a landing page tool first, with some sales capabilities bolted on. But if your workflow is "drive traffic to a landing page, capture leads, and hand them off to an email platform," Leadpages does that better than any all-in-one.

Pricing: Starts at $37/month (Standard) for one site and unlimited landing pages. The Pro plan at $74/month adds A/B testing, online sales, and email trigger links. Annual billing drops costs further.

Pros: Best-in-class landing page templates, fast and intuitive builder, excellent mobile responsiveness, strong conversion analytics, affordable entry price.

Cons: Not a true all-in-one (no course hosting, limited email), requires pairing with an email platform, basic checkout compared to full funnel builders.

Best for: Marketers who prioritize landing page quality and conversion rates, and are happy to pair Leadpages with a separate email marketing tool.

5. GetResponse — Best for Email-First Marketers

Verdict: GetResponse started as an email marketing platform and has expanded into funnels, landing pages, webinars, and ecommerce. If email is the backbone of your marketing and you want solid funnel capabilities on top, GetResponse is a compelling Systeme.io alternative with proven deliverability.

GetResponse has been in the email game for over 25 years, and it shows. Deliverability rates are consistently among the best in the industry. The automation builder is visual and powerful, supporting complex workflows with conditions, actions, and filters. You also get built-in webinar hosting — a feature almost no other all-in-one platform includes at this price point.

The funnel builder (called Conversion Funnels) walks you through creating opt-in funnels, sales funnels, and webinar funnels step by step. It's not as flexible as ClickFunnels or Systeme.io's builder, but for standard funnel types it gets the job done efficiently. The landing page builder has improved significantly and now includes AI-assisted page creation.

Pricing: Free plan available for up to 500 contacts (email only). Paid plans start at $15.60/month (Email Marketing) and scale to $97.60/month (Ecommerce Marketing) for the full feature set. The Marketing Automation plan at $48.40/month is the sweet spot for most users.

Pros: Excellent email deliverability, built-in webinar hosting, free plan available, mature automation workflows, AI-powered email and page tools, competitive pricing.

Cons: Funnel builder is less flexible than dedicated tools, course/membership features are basic, free plan is email-only with a 500-contact cap.

Best for: Marketers who consider email their primary channel, need reliable deliverability, and want funnel and webinar capabilities without paying for a separate platform.

6. Builderall — Best Budget Option

Verdict: Builderall packs an almost absurd number of tools into a single subscription starting under $15/month. If you're on a tight budget and want access to everything from funnel building to app creation, Builderall gives you more features per dollar than anything else on the market.

Builderall's approach is quantity. The platform includes over 40 tools: a website builder, funnel builder, email marketing, chatbots, a booking system, an elearning platform, a blog builder, a browser notification tool, a video hosting platform, and even a design tool. The sheer breadth is impressive — and occasionally overwhelming.

The tradeoff is depth. Individual tools in Builderall rarely match the quality of dedicated alternatives. The funnel builder works but isn't as polished as ClickFunnels. The email tool sends emails but lacks the automation sophistication of GetResponse. The course platform hosts courses but doesn't offer the student experience of Kajabi. If you need one specific thing done really well, a specialized tool will outperform Builderall. But if you need a bit of everything and your budget is tight, it's hard to argue with the value.

Pricing: Starts at $14.90/month (Core plan). The Essentials plan at $29.90/month unlocks most tools. The Premium plan at $69.90/month includes everything plus priority support. All plans include unlimited subscribers.

Pros: Extremely affordable, 40+ tools included, unlimited subscribers on all plans, generous feature set for the price, active development with frequent updates.

Cons: Individual tools lack polish, interface can feel disorganized, documentation is inconsistent, learning curve due to sheer volume of features, occasional performance issues.

Best for: Budget-conscious entrepreneurs who need access to a wide range of marketing tools and are willing to trade some polish for significant cost savings.

7. Kajabi — Best for Premium Courses

Verdict: Kajabi is the premium choice for creators who want their courses, coaching programs, and memberships to feel like a polished, professional product. It costs more than Systeme.io — significantly more — but the student experience and brand perception are in a different league. For a related comparison, see our Kajabi vs Teachable breakdown.

Kajabi combines course hosting, community, coaching, email marketing, landing pages, and podcast hosting into a platform that's designed around the knowledge commerce business model. Every feature is built to support selling and delivering digital products, and the result is a cohesive experience that looks and feels premium out of the box.

The course builder supports video lessons, quizzes, assignments, and community discussions. The student dashboard is clean and intuitive — your customers won't need a tutorial to navigate it. Kajabi also includes a mobile app (branded under your business name on the highest plan), which is a significant differentiator for creators with large audiences.

Where Kajabi trails Systeme.io is obvious: price. The entry point is $149/month, and there's no free plan. But if your course business generates $5,000+ per month in revenue, the upgrade in perceived quality and student retention can easily justify the investment.

Pricing: Starts at $149/month (Basic) for 3 products and 10,000 contacts. The Growth plan at $199/month expands to 15 products and 25,000 contacts. The Pro plan at $399/month adds a custom mobile app and 100 products.

Pros: Best-in-class course experience, professional and modern design, mobile app option, built-in community and coaching tools, strong email marketing, podcast hosting included.

Cons: Most expensive option on this list, no free plan, limited funnel-building capabilities compared to ClickFunnels, transaction fees on the Basic plan.

Best for: Established course creators, coaches, and membership site owners who want a premium brand experience and are generating enough revenue to justify the investment.

How We Chose These Alternatives

We evaluated each platform against Systeme.io across five criteria:

  • Funnel building: Can it create multi-step sales funnels with opt-in pages, sales pages, upsells, and thank-you pages?
  • Email marketing: Does it include automation, segmentation, and reliable deliverability?
  • Course/membership hosting: Can you deliver digital products with a solid student experience?
  • Checkout and payments: Does it process payments natively with support for subscriptions and one-time purchases?
  • Value: How does the total cost compare to what Systeme.io offers, especially considering the free plan?

We also factored in ease of use, customer support quality, and how actively each platform is being developed. For our full take on Systeme.io's pricing tiers, check the dedicated pricing breakdown.

Which Systeme.io Alternative Should You Pick?

The right choice depends entirely on what's driving you away from Systeme.io:

  • Need better funnels? Go with ClickFunnels. It's the specialist.
  • Running an agency? GoHighLevel was built for you.
  • Selling courses with deep automation? Kartra's behavioral triggers are unmatched.
  • Just need great landing pages? Leadpages keeps it simple and effective.
  • Email is your main channel? GetResponse has 25 years of deliverability expertise.
  • On a tight budget? Builderall gives you the most tools for the least money.
  • Want a premium course brand? Kajabi is worth every penny if your revenue supports it.

And if none of these pain points resonate? Systeme.io is still one of the best values in the all-in-one marketing space. Its free plan remains unmatched, and the paid tiers are competitively priced for what you get. Sometimes the best alternative is sticking with what works.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Systeme.io alternative for building funnels?

ClickFunnels is the best Systeme.io alternative if your primary focus is funnel building. It offers more advanced split-testing, funnel templates, and a dedicated funnel workflow. GoHighLevel is another strong option if you also need CRM and agency features.

Is there a free alternative to Systeme.io?

No other all-in-one platform matches Systeme.io's free plan, which includes funnels, email, courses, and automation. GetResponse offers a free tier for email marketing, and Builderall occasionally runs promotional free trials, but neither matches the breadth of Systeme.io's free offering.

Can I migrate from Systeme.io to another platform easily?

Most platforms let you export your email contacts and re-create funnels manually, but there is no one-click migration tool. Kartra and GoHighLevel offer onboarding assistance that can help with the transition. We recommend running both platforms in parallel for 2–4 weeks before fully switching.
