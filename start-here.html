What Tools Does Your Business Actually Need?

A no-BS guide to building your tool stack without overspending.

Start With Your Biggest Bottleneck

Here's the truth most tool review sites won't tell you: you probably don't need 15 different subscriptions. Most businesses — especially early-stage ones — need two or three tools, tops. The rest is noise that drains your budget and splits your attention.

Instead of shopping for tools, start with a question: What is the single biggest thing slowing your business down right now?

Find your answer below, and we'll point you to exactly what you need — nothing more.

Pick Your Path

Path A: "I need to get found online" → SEO Tools

If nobody can find your website on Google, nothing else matters. SEO tools help you figure out what people are searching for, track your rankings, and find technical issues holding your site back.

Our recommendation: Semrush if you're an established business ready to invest in growth. If you're just starting out, Google Search Console is completely free and gives you real data straight from Google.

  • Semrush Review
  • Best Keyword Research Tools

Path B: "I need to build an email list" → Email Marketing

Social media algorithms change. Your email list is the one audience you actually own. Building it early — even before you have much to sell — is one of the smartest moves you can make.

Our recommendation: MailerLite offers the best value with a generous free plan and clean automation tools. If you're a creator (blogger, YouTuber, podcaster), Kit is purpose-built for you.

  • Best Email Marketing Platforms
  • MailerLite vs Mailchimp
  • Kit Review

Path C: "I need to create content faster" → AI Writing Tools

Content marketing works, but it's slow. AI writing tools won't replace a good writer, but they can cut your drafting time dramatically — blog posts, ad copy, product descriptions, email sequences.

Our recommendation: Jasper is the strongest option for teams that need brand voice consistency and collaboration features. If you're a solopreneur on a budget, Writesonic delivers solid output at a lower price.

  • Best AI Writing Tools
  • Jasper vs Copy.ai
  • Writesonic Review

Path D: "I need a website" → Website Builders & Hosting

Your website is your home base. The right builder depends on whether you want full design control, plan to blog heavily, or just need a simple landing page up fast.

Our recommendation: Framer for modern, design-forward sites without touching code. If you're committed to WordPress, skip cheap shared hosting and go with a managed WordPress host — the speed and support difference is night and day.

  • Best Website Builders for Small Business
  • Framer Review
  • Best Managed WordPress Hosting

Path E: "I need to sell online" → E-Commerce & Funnels

Selling physical products and selling digital products are two very different problems. Pick the platform that matches what you're actually selling.

Our recommendation: Shopify is still the gold standard for physical products — inventory, shipping, payments, all handled. For digital products (courses, ebooks, memberships), Systeme.io does it all with a genuinely useful free plan.

  • Best E-Commerce Platforms

Path F: "I need to manage customers" → CRM

Once you're talking to more prospects than you can track in your head (or a spreadsheet), you need a CRM. It keeps every conversation, deal, and follow-up in one place so nothing slips through the cracks.

Our recommendation: HubSpot free CRM is genuinely free for the core features and scales with you. There's no reason to pay for a CRM until you outgrow it.

The Free Stack: $0 to Get Started

Don't have a budget yet? You can build a surprisingly capable tool stack without spending a dime (or close to it):

  • SEO: Google Search Console — free, directly from Google
  • Email Marketing: MailerLite free plan — up to 1,000 subscribers
  • Sales Funnels & Courses: Systeme.io free plan — funnels, email, courses, all included
  • CRM: HubSpot free CRM — contact management, deals, tasks
  • Simple Website: Carrd — $19/year for a clean, fast one-page site

That's a full business stack for under $20 a year. Start here, upgrade only when a tool starts limiting your growth.
