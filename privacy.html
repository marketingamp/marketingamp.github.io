Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

At ToolStackReview, your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to being transparent about our data practices and keeping things straightforward.

Information We Collect

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. Google Analytics collects anonymous, aggregated data including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring websites (how you found us)
  • General geographic location (country and city level, not your exact address)
  • Browser type and device information
  • Operating system

This data is collected in aggregate and cannot be used to personally identify you. We use it solely to understand which content is most helpful and to improve our site.

Email Subscriptions

If you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. Your email address is used only to send you the content you signed up for. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in every email. We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or process:

  • Personal identification details (name, phone number, or physical address) unless you voluntarily provide them via email
  • Payment or financial information
  • Social Security numbers or government-issued IDs
  • Health or medical information

Cookies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and provide information to site owners.

Cookies We Use

ToolStackReview uses the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies: Set by Google Analytics to help us understand site usage patterns. These cookies collect anonymous data and expire after a set period.
  • Functional cookies: Used to remember your preferences, such as cookie consent choices.

Third-Party Cookies

When you click on affiliate links on our site, the third-party websites you visit may set their own cookies. These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of those respective websites, not by this policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit through our links.

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete existing cookies
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies
  • Set preferences for certain websites

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.

How We Use Your Information

The limited data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Understand which content is most useful to our readers
  • Improve site performance and user experience
  • Deliver newsletter content to subscribers who opt in
  • Monitor site health and troubleshoot technical issues

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to anyone. We may share anonymous, aggregated analytics data that cannot identify any individual user. The only third parties with access to any data are:

  • Google Analytics: For website usage analysis (anonymous data only)
  • Email service provider: For delivering newsletters to subscribers

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data we collect. Our site uses HTTPS encryption to secure data transmitted between your browser and our servers. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

ToolStackReview contains links to third-party websites, including affiliate partner sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any website you visit.

Children's Privacy

ToolStackReview is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
