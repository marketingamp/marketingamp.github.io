# ToolStackReview — Directory & Aggregator Submissions

Submit to these free directories for backlinks and referral traffic. Each one takes 5-10 minutes.

## Tier 1: High-Value (Do These First)

### AlternativeTo (alternativeto.net)
- **What:** User-driven software recommendation site with strong DA
- **Action:** Create listings for tools we review. Add ToolStackReview as a source/review link
- **URL:** https://alternativeto.net/how-to-add-an-application/
- **Notes:** Focus on Systeme.io, GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels — these have active alternative pages

### SaaSHub (saashub.com)
- **What:** SaaS discovery platform with comparison features
- **Action:** Submit ToolStackReview as a resource. Add reviews for tools we cover
- **URL:** https://www.saashub.com/submit
- **Notes:** Good for backlinks and appears in Google for "[tool] alternatives" queries

### Product Hunt (producthunt.com)
- **What:** Tech product launch platform, very high DA
- **Action:** Launch ToolStackReview as a product — "Independent SaaS review site for marketers"
- **URL:** https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new
- **Notes:** Best launched on a Tuesday/Wednesday. Create a compelling tagline. Can drive significant one-day traffic

### G2 (g2.com)
- **What:** Enterprise software review site with massive organic traffic
- **Action:** Submit reviews for tools we've tested. Include ToolStackReview link in your reviewer profile
- **URL:** https://www.g2.com/
- **Notes:** Reviews should be genuine and detailed. G2 verifies reviewers. Profile backlinks are nofollow but referral traffic is real

### Capterra (capterra.com)
- **What:** Gartner-owned software review platform
- **Action:** Write reviews for tools we cover. Profile links provide backlinks
- **URL:** https://www.capterra.com/reviews/new
- **Notes:** Similar to G2 — focus on genuine, detailed reviews

## Submission Tips

1. **Use consistent branding:** Name is "ToolStackReview", tagline is "Independent SaaS tool reviews for marketers and business owners"
2. **Description template:** "ToolStackReview provides hands-on reviews, comparisons, and pricing guides for AI tools, email marketing platforms, landing page builders, SEO tools, and VPN services. Independent, affiliate-transparent, and focused on helping you pick the right tools."
3. **URL:** https://marketingamp.github.io
4. **Category:** Technology / Software Reviews / Marketing Tools
5. **Screenshots:** Take clean screenshots of the homepage and a sample article
6. **Do 2-3 submissions per day** — spacing them out looks more natural and prevents fatigue
