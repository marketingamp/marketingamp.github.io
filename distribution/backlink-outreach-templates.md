## Outreach Tips

1. **Send 3-5 emails per day max** — quality over quantity
2. **Always personalize** — mention something specific about their article or site
3. **Follow up once** after 5-7 days if no response. Never follow up more than once
4. **Target sites with DA 20-60** — they're more likely to respond than huge sites
5. **Find contacts using:** site's About/Contact page, LinkedIn, Hunter.io, or Twitter bio
6. **Track everything** in the spreadsheet below

## How to Find Outreach Targets

1. **Google:** "[your topic] resources" or "[your topic] useful links"
2. **Ahrefs/Semrush free tools:** Find who links to competitors' content
3. **Google:** "write for us" + [your niche] for guest post targets
4. **Check competitors' backlink profiles** using free tools like Ubersuggest
5. **Look for roundup articles** ranking for keywords you target
